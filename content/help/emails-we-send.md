Underground Aquarium only emails you about things that involve you directly: a message from another member, your Society membership, a new bubble tier, or alerts about a shop you run. We don't send members newsletters. This guide lists every email and explains what you can switch off.

## Which emails will I get as a member?
| Email | When it's sent |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-up confirmation | Right after you register with an email and password |
| Password reset link | When you ask for one on **Forgot your password?** |
| New message | Every time another member messages you |
| New bubble tier | When your bubbles carry you into a new tier |
| Bubbles from our team | When our team awards or removes bubbles |
| Society and club emails | Application approved or declined, honorary membership, dues requests, dues reminders, dues receipts, club invites |
| Shop alerts | Only if you manage a claimed shop: new reviews, listing fix reports, milestones, weekly report |
| Notification emails | Forum replies, comments, mentions, trophies, shop updates and more that you haven't seen on the site yet. Bundled at most once an hour, or once a day, as you choose |

Each email ends with a line explaining why you received it, like "You're receiving this because another member messaged you on Underground Aquarium."

## The new message email
Every time someone sends you a message on the site, you get an email.

- **Subject:** "[Name] sent you a message" for a direct message, or "[Name] messaged you about "[listing title]"" when it's about one of your classified ads.
- **Inside:** "New message from [Name]", a preview of the message (up to about 300 characters), and a **Read and reply** button that opens the conversation.

Reply on the site, not by replying to the email. New messages don't appear in the bell; they show on the Messages icon. See [Message notifications](/help/message-notifications).

## Bubble emails
- **"You reached [tier name]"**: sent once when your bubbles carry you into a new tier. It shows the tier, your balance and a **See your profile** button. See [Bubble tiers and milestones](/help/bubble-tiers-and-milestones).
- **"You earned [number] bubbles"** or **"Your bubbles were adjusted"**: sent when our team adds or removes bubbles by hand. It shows the amount, the reason, your new balance and your current tier.

Everyday bubble earns (logging in, posting and so on) don't send email.

## Society and club emails
If you apply to or belong to The Society (or a club), you may get:

- **Approved:** "You're approved for the [club]. One step left." with a **Pay [amount] and activate** button, or "Welcome to the [club]" if there are no dues to pay.
- **Application declined:** "About your [club] application", a short, kind note saying your application couldn't be approved this time and that you're welcome to apply again, with an **Apply again** button. Your account, classifieds and tools keep working as before.
- **Honorary membership:** "You've been made an Honorary Lifetime Member of the [club]".
- **Dues request:** "Membership dues for [club]", sent when an officer asks you to pay, with a **Pay your dues** button.
- **Dues reminders:** 10 days before your membership's paid-through date ("Your [club] membership expires in 10 days"), 3 days before ("...expires in 3 days") and on the day ("...expires today"), each with a **Renew now** button. For The Society, **Renew now** opens the renewal box on the membership page. The 10-day email explains that renewing early adds a full year on top of your current one, so you don't lose any days. If you still haven't renewed 3 days after the date, you get "Your membership has lapsed", which says your records are safe, with a **Renew your membership** button. Nothing renews automatically: you pay by hand each year.
- **Dues receipt:** "Your [club] dues receipt", with the amount and the date your membership is active through. Keep it as your receipt.
- **Club invite:** "You're invited to join [club]", with a **Join the club** button, when an officer invites your email address.

Dues reminders aren't sent to family members whose dues are covered by a main member, or to the club's owner. See [Society dues and renewal](/help/society-dues-and-renewal).

## Shop owner emails
If you manage a claimed fish store, you get an email for each shop alert:

- **New review:** "New [rating]-star review for [shop]", with a **Reply to this review** button.
- **Listing fix report:** "A shopper says [what's wrong]", with a **Check your listing** button.
- **Milestone:** "[shop] just passed [number] views" or "[number] people now follow [shop]".
- **Weekly report:** "[shop]: [number] views this week", sent Monday mornings (Pacific time), with an **Open your shop dashboard** button.

Each one says at the bottom: "You're getting this because you run [shop] on Underground Aquarium," with a link to notification settings. See [Shop notifications and emails](/help/shop-notifications-and-emails).

## Which emails can I turn off?
**You choose by category.** On [Notifications](/notifications), open **Choose what you hear about, on the site and by email**. Pick how often under **Emails** (As it happens, Once a day or Never), then use the envelope switch on each category: messages, comments, mentions, forum replies, likes, trophies and new tiers, bubbles, your contributions, shop updates, review replies, the four shop alerts, Society, events and site news. Every notification email also has a one-click **stop notification emails** link at the bottom.

**Always sent:** sign-in emails, Society dues requests, reminders and receipts, and notices about your own account.

If you want something else stopped, email support@undergroundaquarium.com and we'll take care of it. Be aware that when our team blocks an address by hand, that stops all of our emails to it, including message alerts and dues reminders.

## Is there an unsubscribe link?
Only on outreach emails, which go to shops listed in our store directory. Those have an **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom.

Clicking it takes the address off our marketing and outreach list straight away, with no login and no confirmation screen. You'll see "You're unsubscribed" and "[address] won't get any more marketing or outreach emails from Underground Aquarium. If you have an account, emails about your account and your messages will still arrive." You'll also get one short email confirming it. Clicking the link twice doesn't send a second confirmation.

If the page says "That link didn't work", email support@undergroundaquarium.com and we'll take you off by hand.

## What happens if I unsubscribe or mark an email as spam?
Unsubscribing or marking one of our emails as spam stops only marketing and outreach email (campaigns and shop outreach). Everything about your own account keeps arriving: sign-in emails, message emails, Society dues reminders and receipts, and shop alert emails.

It's different if emails to your address bounce (the address doesn't work) or our team blocks the address by hand. Then our system stops sending to it entirely, including message alerts, dues reminders and receipts. Your account keeps working, and you still see everything in the bell and on the Messages icon.

To start getting emails again after a bounce or block, email support@undergroundaquarium.com from that address and ask us to turn email back on.

## Where do emails come from and what do they look like?
Emails come from "Underground Aquarium" and have a dark header with the UNDERGROUND AQUARIUM wordmark. Buttons in them always point to undergroundaquarium.com.

We never ask for your password by email. If you get something asking for your password or payment details that claims to be us, don't click it. Forward it to support@undergroundaquarium.com.

## Do I get an email for forum replies, likes or comments?
Yes, if you haven't seen them on the site within about 10 minutes. Forum replies, comments, mentions and most other notices are emailed, bundled so you get at most one email an hour (or one a day if you choose). Likes and bubbles start switched off for email because they're frequent. Change any of it in your [notification settings](/notifications#settings). See [Notifications](/help/notifications).

## Common problems

**I'm not getting any emails.**
Check your spam or junk folder and any "Promotions" or "Updates" tabs. Make sure the email on [Account & data](/account) is right. Unsubscribing or marking an email as spam doesn't stop account or message emails. But if emails to your address ever bounced, it may be blocked from all our emails; email support@undergroundaquarium.com to turn it back on.

**I didn't get my sign-up confirmation email.**
Wait a couple of minutes and check spam. If you typed the wrong address, use **Use a different email** on the **Check your inbox** screen and register again. See [Confirming your email](/help/confirming-your-email).

**I didn't get a password reset email.**
For privacy, the site says a link is on its way whether or not an account exists for that address. Make sure you used the address you signed up with. If you signed up with Google, you don't have a password; use **Continue with Google**. See [Signing in and passwords](/help/signing-in-and-passwords).

**I get an email for every single message.**
Message emails can't be switched off in settings, and unsubscribing doesn't stop them. If they're too much, email support@undergroundaquarium.com, but note that blocking your address stops all our emails to you.

**I turned off Bubbles but still got a tier email.**
The **Bubbles** switch controls the bell only. Tier emails still go out.

**I get shop emails but I don't own the shop anymore.**
Switch off the **Your shop** alerts on [Notifications](/notifications), and email support@undergroundaquarium.com so we can remove you from the shop.

**An email arrived late.**
Occasionally we pause and queue email for a short time; queued emails go out automatically afterward.

**I want to stop outreach emails to my store.**
Click **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of the email, or reply and ask. See [Stopping shop emails](/help/stopping-shop-emails).
