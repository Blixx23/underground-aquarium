An alert tells you the moment someone near you posts an ad that matches what you're after, so you don't have to keep checking. Alerts are free. You need an account to save one, because that's how we reach you.

## How do I make an alert?
1. On any classifieds page, press **Get alerts** (or **Alert me when it's posted** when nothing matches your search).
2. If you're not signed in, sign in or join. You come straight back with the alert form open.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **What are you looking for?** Words that must all be in the ad, like "cherry shrimp", "oscar" or "75 gallon". Leave it empty to get everything in a category.
   - **Category**: one category, or any.
   - **Where**: filled in from the area you're browsing, or press **Use my location**, or pick your state and area.
   - **How far**: within 25, 50, 100 or 150 miles.
   - **Also tell me about Wanted posts**: tick it to hear about people looking to buy, too.
4. Press **Save alert**.

You need either some words or a category, so you don't get every ad posted near you.

## How will I be told?
With a bell on the site, and an email unless you've turned classified alert emails off in your notification settings (the **Classified alerts** switch). If several ads match at once, you get one notice listing them, not one each. You never hear about the same ad twice, never about your own ads, and never about ads from someone you've blocked or who has blocked you.

New ads are checked every 10 minutes.

## Is my location saved?
Only the metro area, like "Sacramento, CA", never your exact spot. **Use my location** finds your nearest area in your browser and saves just that.

## How are Wanted posts matched?
If you post a **Wanted** ad, we watch for ads within about 60 miles in the same category that look like what you asked for (at least two of the main words in your Wanted title, or the only one). You get a bell when one is posted.

It works the other way too: if you're selling something and someone near you posts a Wanted ad that looks like it, you get a bell saying someone is looking, with a link to their Wanted post so you can message them.

Matches are a best guess from the words used, so check the ad before you go.

## How do I pause or remove an alert?
Open **My alerts** from your account menu (or the **My alerts** button on My listings, or go to /my/alerts). Each alert shows what it watches, where, and when it last matched. Press **Pause** to stop it for now, **Turn on** to start it again, or the bin to remove it.

You can have up to 10 alerts on at once.

## Common problems
**I'm not getting emails.** Check the **Classified alerts** switch in your notification settings, and your spam folder. The bell on the site always shows matches.

**I'm getting ads I don't want.** Add more words to the alert or pick a category. Every word has to be in the ad, so "red cherry shrimp" is narrower than "shrimp".

**It says alerts aren't switched on yet.** The feature is still being set up. Try again later.
