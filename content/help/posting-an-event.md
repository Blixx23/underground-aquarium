Anyone with a free account can post an aquarium event on [Events](/events): a swap, auction, club meeting, meetup, sale or online get-together. Posting is free. Community events are reviewed by our team before they go public; events posted for a fish store you manage go live right away.

## How do I post an event?
1. Sign in and go to [Events](/events).
2. Click **Submit an event** (top right). This opens [Submit an event](/events/submit).
3. Fill in the form. Only **Event title** and **Starts** are required.
4. Click **Submit event**. It shows "Submitting..." while it saves.

If you're signed out, the submit page says "Please sign in to post an event." with a **Sign in** button.

## What does each field on the event form mean?
- **Posting as** (only if you manage a published fish store): choose **Community event (you)** or one of your shops, shown as "[shop name] (your shop)." The hint underneath says whether it will be reviewed or publish right away.
- **Event title** (required). For example "Spring Frag Swap." Links aren't allowed.
- **Description** (optional but important). Put everything people need right here: what's happening, who should come, times, prices, what to bring. Line breaks are kept. Links aren't allowed.
- **Starts** (required): date and time.
- **Ends (optional)**: date and time.
- **This is an online event**: tick this for a virtual event. The address fields are replaced by **Event link (optional)**.
- **Event link (optional)**, online events only: the meeting link people use to join. It appears on the event page as a **join link**.
- **Venue name**, **Address**, **City**, **State** and **ZIP**, in-person events only, all optional. For example "Rocklin Community Center." Use the 2-letter state code, like CA.
- **Capacity (optional)**: the maximum number of people who can RSVP as going. Leave it blank for unlimited.
- **Cover image (optional)**: click **Click to upload a cover image** and choose a photo.

The **Submit event** button stays grayed out until you've entered a title and a start time.

## What time zone does my event use?
When you first submit, the start and end times are saved in your device's time zone. So if you're in California posting a Texas auction and you type 9:00 AM, it's saved as 9:00 AM Pacific.

To fix this, after posting open the event, click **Edit event** and choose the right **Time zone** (Eastern, Central, Mountain, Arizona, Pacific, Alaska or Hawaii). In the edit panel, the times you enter are local to the event, so a 9:30 AM Ohio auction still reads 9:30 AM when edited from California. See [Editing and deleting events](/help/editing-and-deleting-events).

## What happens after I submit a community event?
You'll see "Thanks, your event was submitted!" and "We review community submissions before they go public. You'll see it on the events page once it's approved." Click **Back to events** to return.

Until it's approved, the event isn't on the public list, and its page shows "This event is pending review and isn't public yet." The site doesn't send a notification or email when your event is approved, so check [Events](/events) for it. After approval, the events list shows "Community event" as the host, and the event page shows your name (or username).

## How long does event review take?
The site doesn't promise a set time. If your event still isn't showing after a reasonable wait, or the event date is getting close, email support@undergroundaquarium.com with the event title and date.

## How do I post an event for my fish store?
If you've claimed a fish store listing and it's published, the form shows a **Posting as** menu.

1. Choose your shop from **Posting as**.
2. Fill in the rest of the form and click **Submit event**.

Shop events skip review: you'll see "Your event is live!" and "It's now published on the events page," with a **View event** button. Your store's name shows as the host and links to your store page. You (and anyone else who manages that store listing) can edit it later. If you don't see **Posting as**, your store claim isn't approved yet or the listing isn't published; see [Claiming your store](/help/claiming-your-store).

## How do I add a cover image to my event?
Click **Click to upload a cover image** in the form and pick a photo. JPG, PNG and iPhone HEIC photos all work; HEIC is converted to JPG automatically. A preview appears, with **Remove image** underneath if you change your mind.

Cover images display in a wide shape (about 1200 by 630), so a landscape photo works best. Without a cover image, the events list shows a colored date card and the event page shows the Underground Aquarium party tank picture.

## How do I post a recurring event, like a monthly meeting?
Each event has one date. Post a separate event for each meeting. After a meeting is posted and approved, open it, click **Edit event** and set **Type** to **Meeting**.

## Can I post an online event?
Yes. Tick **This is an online event**, and optionally paste the meeting link into **Event link (optional)**. The event page shows **Online event** with a **join link**, and the list shows **Online**. The meeting link goes in that field only; links in the title or description aren't allowed.

## Can I add a link to tickets or a website?
No. Links aren't allowed in the title or description (see [Event rules](/help/event-rules)). Write out ticket prices and how to get tickets in the description. Online events can use the separate **Event link** field for the meeting link.

## Is posting an event free?
Yes. Posting and RSVPing are free.

## Common problems
**"A title and start date/time are required."** Enter an **Event title** and pick a **Starts** date and time.

**"Links aren't allowed in event descriptions. Put all the details right here on Underground Aquarium."** Your title or description contains a web address or something that looks like one (for example a word ending in .com, .org or .net). Remove it and write the details out. Mentioning undergroundaquarium.com is fine. See [Event rules](/help/event-rules).

**"Couldn't submit the event. Please check your details and try again."** The event didn't save. Check your details, then try again.

**"Couldn't upload that image. Try a JPG or PNG."** The photo didn't upload. Try a JPG or PNG, or a smaller photo.

**"Please sign in again."** Your session ended while uploading. Sign in and try again.

**My event isn't on the events page.** Community events wait for review first. If it's approved and still missing, open it, click **Edit event** and make sure **Show on the public events page** is ticked. Events also drop off the list about 12 hours after they start.

**My event shows the wrong time.** It was saved in your device's time zone. Open it, click **Edit event**, pick the event's **Time zone** and re-enter the times.

**I can't find my pending event.** You aren't given a link to a pending event. Once it's approved it appears on [Events](/events). If you need to change something before then, email support@undergroundaquarium.com.
