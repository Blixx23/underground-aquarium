Underground Aquarium used to host a directory of separate local aquarium clubs. That directory has been retired. The site now hosts one national club instead: the Underground Aquarium Society, open to hobbyists in every state, with no chapters.

## Where did the clubs directory go?
The multi-club directory is gone. Every old clubs address (the clubs list, "discover," "start" and "new club" pages, and anything else starting with /clubs) now takes you to [The Society](/society). Links to other clubs' main pages (addresses like /c/club-name) also go to the Society.

The Society is one membership for the whole country, with a judged Breeder Award Program, a permanent member number and signed certificates. See [The Underground Aquarium Society](/help/the-society-overview).

## Can I start my own club on Underground Aquarium?
No. Creating new clubs is no longer available, and the start-a-club pages send you to [The Society](/society). If you run a local club somewhere else, you can still post its meetings and events on Underground Aquarium for free (see below).

## Where did club features go?
- **Club directory and club pages:** replaced by [The Society](/society) and its [membership page](/c/underground-aquarium-society).
- **Joining a club:** you apply to the Society instead. See [Joining the Society](/help/joining-the-society).
- **Club dues:** Society dues are the only dues collected on the site. See [Society dues and renewal](/help/society-dues-and-renewal).
- **Family memberships:** retired. Each person has their own membership.
- **Club award programs and breeder points:** now the Society's [Breeder Award Program](/help/breeder-award-program). Old Society awards links redirect: the awards standings go to the [Leaderboard](/society/leaderboard), the submit form goes to [Open a spawn log](/society/breeder/new), and the review page goes to the member area.
- **Club events and meetings:** local meetings are posted on the site-wide [Events](/events) page. The Society's own events are listed there too.
- **Club invitations:** Society officers can still invite people by email. See [Joining the Society](/help/joining-the-society).

## How do I find a local aquarium club or meeting near me?
Use [Events](/events) and click **Find events near me**. Hobbyists, clubs and shops post meetings, swaps and auctions there, and the list re-sorts by distance from you. See [Finding events](/help/finding-events).

Local fish stores are also a good way to hear about clubs in your area. Browse [Shops Near Me](/stores?near=1).

## How do I join a club on Underground Aquarium?
The Society is the club you can join here.

1. Go to [The Society](/society) and click **Apply for membership**.
2. Sign in or create a free account.
3. Fill in the application and click **Submit application**.

Officers review applications. Once you're approved, you pay dues and step into the member area.

## How do I post my local club's meeting as an event?
1. Sign in, go to [Events](/events) and click **Submit an event**.
2. Enter an **Event title** like "Monthly Club Meeting."
3. Write the **Description** in full: who should come, what happens, any cost, what to bring. Links aren't allowed, so write everything out.
4. Set when it **Starts** (and optionally **Ends**) and fill in the **Venue name**, **Address**, **City**, **State** and **ZIP**.
5. Click **Submit event**. Our team reviews community events before they go public.

After it's approved, open the event, click **Edit event**, set **Type** to **Meeting**, and check the **Time zone**. Each meeting is its own event, so post one for each date. See [Posting an event](/help/posting-an-event).

## Where are the Society's own events?
Events the Society hosts appear on [Events](/events) with "Underground Aquarium Society" as the host. There's also a Society events list at /c/underground-aquarium-society/events. Society officers add and edit those events there. Some Society events can be set to show only on that list instead of the public events page.

## What happened to my old club page link?
Old club page links open [The Society](/society). If you're a Society member in good standing, Society links take you straight into your member area. If you had a question about a membership you held in a club on the old directory, email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

## Common problems
**My bookmark to a club page now shows the Society.** That's expected. The club directory is retired and all club links point to the Society.

**I clicked an old club awards link and ended up on the Leaderboard or Overview.** Old award pages redirect to their Society replacements. The review page only opens for Society judges; everyone else lands on Overview.

**"Family memberships are no longer offered. Each member has their own membership."** Family plans were retired. Apply for your own membership.

**I can't find a club in my area.** There are no local chapters. Check [Events](/events) for meetings near you, or ask at a local fish store.
