You can tag another member in what you write by typing **@** and their username, like **@salmon868**. Their name turns into a link to their profile, and they get a notification that you mentioned them.

## Where can I mention people?
- Forum threads and forum replies, including when you edit a post
- Feed posts, including when you edit a post
- Comments on feed posts, tanks and listings
- Comments on a tank's own page

Messages don't need mentions. The person you're messaging already gets told.

## How do I mention someone?
1. In any of the boxes above, type **@** and the start of their username or name.
2. A list of matching members pops up under the box.
3. Pick the person with your mouse or finger, or use the arrow keys and press **Enter** or **Tab**. Press **Escape** to close the list.

The username goes in with a space after it, ready for you to keep typing. You can also type the full username yourself, like **@Fish_Guy**. Capital letters don't matter.

## What does the person I mention see?
A notification that says **[Your name] mentioned you**, with the start of what you wrote. Tapping it opens the post or comment.

## When doesn't a mention send a notification?
- You mention yourself.
- The username doesn't belong to anyone, or the account was deleted or suspended.
- The person has blocked you.
- The person turned mentions off in their notification settings.
- They already got a reply or comment notice for the same thing (for example, you replied to their comment and tagged them too). One notice is enough.
- They were already told about this post. Editing a post only notifies people you newly tag.
- It's in the Shop Owners Lounge, which most members can't open.
- The @ is part of an email address, a web address, or code in a forum post.

You can tag up to 10 people in one post or comment. Anyone past that still gets a link, but no notification.

## Can I stop getting mention notifications?
Yes. On the [Notifications](/notifications#settings) page, open **Choose what you get notified about** and switch off **Mentions**. You can also use the **…** menu on any mention notification and choose **Turn off mentions**. See [Notifications](/help/notifications).

## Someone is tagging me to bother me
Block them from their profile and their mentions stop reaching you. See [Blocking people](/help/blocking-people). If it's harassment, report it.
