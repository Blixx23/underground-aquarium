If you'd rather we didn't email your shop, you can unsubscribe in one click. Here's exactly what that does, and what it doesn't do, so there are no surprises.

## How do I unsubscribe my shop from Underground Aquarium emails?
1. Open any email we sent to your shop.
2. At the bottom, tap **Unsubscribe** ("Unsubscribe to stop these emails.").
3. A page opens saying "You're unsubscribed" and "[your address] won't get any more marketing or outreach emails from Underground Aquarium. If you have an account, emails about your account and your messages will still arrive."

That's it. There's no sign-in, no form and no "are you sure" screen. Many email apps also show their own unsubscribe button at the top of our emails, and it does the same thing.

## Will I get a confirmation?
Yes, one short email with the subject "You've been unsubscribed", saying you've been unsubscribed from our marketing and outreach emails and won't get any more of them. It also says that if you have an account with us, emails about your account and your messages will still arrive. It's only sent the first time, so clicking the link twice won't send two.

## What happens to my shop's page when I unsubscribe?
Nothing. Your shop stays listed in the directory, and its page stays up so customers can still find your address, phone, hours and directions. Shoppers can still read and leave reviews, post stock sightings, follow the shop and suggest fixes. Unsubscribing only changes what we send to your inbox.

If the page has details that are wrong, you can still fix them (see below).

## Does unsubscribing stop all emails, or just the outreach?
Just the outreach. Unsubscribing stops marketing and outreach email to that address: the emails about your free page and any other campaign-style email. It also takes your shop out of every outreach series, and anything of that kind already waiting to go out is cancelled.

These still arrive, because they're about your own account and activity:

- Shop alert emails: new review emails, listing report emails, milestone emails and the Monday weekly report, if you claim the shop with that address
- Account emails, such as sign-in and password emails
- Message emails and Society dues emails, if you have an account with that address

In-app notifications also keep arriving in the bell if you have an account. To get fewer shop alerts, sign in and switch off the ones you don't want under **Choose what you get notified about** on your [Notifications](/notifications) page. See [Shop notifications and emails](/help/shop-notifications-and-emails).

The one exception: if emails to your address bounce (the address doesn't work) or our team blocks it, nothing at all is sent to it.

## I unsubscribed but now I want emails again. Can I undo it?
Yes, but not by yourself on the site. Email support@undergroundaquarium.com from the address you unsubscribed, say you'd like outreach emails turned back on, and we'll do it. You don't need to do this to get shop alerts or account emails, since unsubscribing never stopped those. If you were thinking about claiming your shop, mention that and we'll send you a claim link.

## Can I unsubscribe and still claim my shop later?
Yes. Unsubscribing doesn't block claiming. Find your shop on [Fish stores](/stores), tap **Claim it** and follow [Claiming your store](/help/claiming-your-store). Shop alert emails still reach the unsubscribed address once you've claimed the shop, because unsubscribing only stops marketing and outreach.

## Can I remove my shop from the directory?
The directory lists independent fish stores as a public resource, so pages stay up even when a shop unsubscribes. If something about the page is a real problem (it's not a fish store, it's closed for good, or the details are wrong and you'd like help fixing them), email support@undergroundaquarium.com and tell us what's going on. If the shop has closed, a shopper or you can also report it with **Suggest a fix** and **Closed for good**.

## How do I stop emails if I'm the owner and already claimed?
You have two choices:

- **Fewer shop alert emails:** sign in, go to [Notifications](/notifications), open **Choose what you get notified about** and switch off any of **Your shop: new reviews**, **Your shop: listing reports**, **Your shop: milestones** and **Your shop: weekly report**. This keeps other email working.
- **No marketing or outreach:** use an **Unsubscribe** link. This stops outreach only. Shop alerts, account emails and message emails still arrive, so use the switches above for those.

If you want no email from us at all, email support@undergroundaquarium.com and ask.

Shop alert emails themselves don't carry an unsubscribe link. Their footer links to your notification settings instead.

## I didn't sign up. Why am I getting emails?
Your shop is in our free directory, and we found your email address published on your shop's own website or listing. We email shops to let them know their page exists and how to claim it. See [Shop notifications and emails](/help/shop-notifications-and-emails).

## Common problems
**"That link didn't work."** The unsubscribe link was incomplete or changed (often from being copied, forwarded or wrapped). Email support@undergroundaquarium.com and we'll take you off the list by hand.

**I unsubscribed but got one more email.** Check the date. The confirmation email ("You've been unsubscribed") is expected. Shop alerts, account emails and message emails are expected too, because unsubscribing only stops marketing and outreach. If you got another outreach email after unsubscribing, forward it to support@undergroundaquarium.com.

**I unsubscribed but I still get review alerts or the weekly report.** That's expected: unsubscribing only stops marketing and outreach. Switch off the shop alerts you don't want in your notification settings.

**I don't get review alerts.** Unsubscribing doesn't cause this. Check the **Your shop** switches under **Choose what you get notified about** and your spam folder. If emails to that address ever bounced, nothing can be sent to it: email support@undergroundaquarium.com to fix it.

**Emails are going to an old or shared shop address.** Email support@undergroundaquarium.com with the address you want removed.
