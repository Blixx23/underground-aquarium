Shoppers help keep the directory honest. On a shop page nobody from the shop has claimed yet, **Suggest a fix** tells us when the details are wrong.

Claimed pages (marked **Owner managed**) are kept up by the shop itself, so they don't have **Suggest a fix**. If a claimed page is wrong, call the shop or email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

**Spotted in stock** has been retired. To see what a shop has in, check the shop's latest posts on its page, or call ahead.

## How do I report wrong store information?
1. Open the shop's page.
2. In the column beside the posts (on a phone, under the **Intro** box), or on the **About** tab, tap **Something wrong here? Suggest a fix**.
3. Sign in if asked. Signed out, the box says "Sign in to send a fix."
4. Pick what's wrong from the menu.
5. Describe the correct details in the box.
6. Tap **Send**, or **Cancel** to close it.

You'll see "Thanks! We'll check it and update the page."

## What can I report with Suggest a fix?
The menu has seven choices:

- **Hours are wrong** (the box suggests a format like "Tue to Sat 11 to 7, Sun 12 to 5, closed Mondays")
- **Closed for good**
- **Moved to a new address** (the box asks for the "New address")
- **Phone number is wrong**
- **Website is wrong**
- **Name is wrong**
- **Something else**

For every choice, write what the page should say. Your note needs at least 2 characters and can be up to 1,000. **Send** stays grayed out until you've typed something.

## What happens after I suggest a fix?
Nothing on the page changes right away. Your report goes to our team to check, and we update the page by hand.

You won't get a notification when the page is updated. Check back on the shop's page.

## Can I see or edit a fix I already sent?
No. Once sent, a fix can't be viewed, edited or withdrawn from the site. If you made a mistake, send another one with the right details, or email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

## Common problems
**I can't find Suggest a fix.** The page is claimed (it says **Owner managed**). Call the shop, or email support@undergroundaquarium.com with the shop's name and what's wrong.

**Send is grayed out.** Type at least 2 characters describing the correct details.

**I reported a fix but the page hasn't changed.** Fixes are checked by hand. If it's urgent (for example the shop has closed), email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

**Where did Spotted in stock go?** It's been retired. Shops now post their own restocks and new arrivals on their page.
