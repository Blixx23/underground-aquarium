Every classified ad has two separate choices: an **ad type** (For sale, Free or Wanted) and a **category** (what the item is). Picking both well is how the right people find your ad.

## What categories can I choose from?
There are 12 categories. The short line under each is what the site shows on its category tiles:

| Category | What goes here |
|---|---|
| **Freshwater Fish** | Community fish, cichlids, bettas, fry |
| **Saltwater & Coral** | Reef fish, frags, colonies, live rock |
| **Shrimp, Snails & Inverts** | Neocaridina, caridina, nerites, crabs |
| **Aquatic Plants** | Trimmings, rare stems, tissue culture |
| **Tanks & Stands** | Glass, acrylic, rimless, stands, lids |
| **Filters, Lights & Equipment** | Canisters, lights, heaters, CO2, pumps |
| **Hardscape & Decor** | Driftwood, stone, substrate, backgrounds |
| **Food & Water Care** | Frozen, live cultures, ferts, test kits |
| **DIY & 3D-Printed** | Homemade gear, printed parts, tools |
| **Free Stuff** | Giveaways, extra fry, spare gear |
| **Wanted / ISO** | Looking for something specific |
| **Everything Else** | Anything that doesn't fit above |

You pick one category per ad. There are no subcategories.

## Which category should I pick?
Pick the category that describes **what the item is**. Buyers filter by category, so an Amano shrimp colony belongs in **Shrimp, Snails & Inverts** even if you're giving it away.

Some tips:
- A full setup (tank, stand, filter, light) usually fits best in **Tanks & Stands**. Mention the extras in the title.
- Fish and plants sold together: pick the category for the main thing, and list the rest in the description.
- Live rock and coral frags go in **Saltwater & Coral**.
- Live food cultures (like daphnia or microworms) go in **Food & Water Care**.
- Use **Everything Else** only when nothing else fits, since fewer people filter to it.

## What's the difference between For sale, Free and Wanted?
You choose the ad type with the three buttons under **What are you posting?** on the form.

- **For sale**: you're selling. Your ad shows your price, or "Contact for price" if you leave the price empty.
- **Free**: you're giving something away or rehoming it. There's no price box. The ad shows **Free** where the price goes and gets a green **Free** tag.
- **Wanted**: you're looking for something. There's no price and no condition. The ad gets a blue **Wanted** tag, and cards show "Wanted" where a price would be. When you find it, the button says **Mark as found** instead of Mark as sold.

For sale is the default when you open the form.

## Should a free item go in the Free Stuff category or use the Free ad type?
They're two separate settings, and they don't set each other.

- The **Free** ad type is what makes your ad show "Free" and the green Free tag.
- The **Free Stuff** category is just a category, like any other. An ad in Free Stuff that's set to **For sale** with a price still shows that price.

The best approach for most giveaways: choose the **Free** ad type, and pick the category for what the item actually is (for example **Aquatic Plants** for trimmings). Buyers browsing plants will see it, and the Free tag still stands out. Use the **Free Stuff** category for mixed giveaways that don't fit anywhere else.

Also: a For sale ad with a price of 0 shows as Free with the Free tag, the same as a Free ad.

## Should a wanted ad go in the Wanted / ISO category or use the Wanted ad type?
Same idea. They're independent.

- The **Wanted** ad type is what adds the blue Wanted tag, hides the price and condition, and changes the button to **Mark as found**.
- The **Wanted / ISO** category is just a category. If you pick it but leave the ad type on **For sale**, your ad won't get the Wanted tag and will show a price or "Contact for price", which confuses people.

For a wanted ad, always choose the **Wanted** ad type. Then pick either the category of the thing you want (so sellers of that thing see it, for example **Tanks & Stands** for "Looking for a 40 gallon breeder") or **Wanted / ISO**.

## What does Condition mean, and when does it show?
**Condition (optional)** lets you say what shape an item is in. The choices are:

- **Not specified** (the default)
- **New**
- **Like new**
- **Good**
- **Fair**
- **For parts**

Condition only appears for non-living items on For sale and Free ads. It's hidden when:
- The category is **Freshwater Fish**, **Saltwater & Coral**, **Shrimp, Snails & Inverts** or **Aquatic Plants**, because "New" or "For parts" doesn't mean much for a living thing.
- The ad type is **Wanted**.

If you had a condition picked and then switch to a live category or to Wanted, the condition is dropped when you save. When set, condition shows on your listing page as "Condition: Good" and on listing cards next to your town.

## How do categories show up when people browse?
On an area page and on the nationwide [Every listing](/listings) page, buyers see a row of category buttons ("pills") above the listings:

- **All** comes first, with the total number of ads.
- After that, only categories that have at least one ad in that area appear, each with its count. Empty categories are hidden.
- Tapping a pill shows only that category, and the line above the grid reads, for example, "4 listings in Aquatic Plants".

Each listing card also shows its category in the top corner, plus a **Wanted** or **Free** tag in the other corner when it applies. See [Searching and filtering listings](/help/searching-and-filtering-listings).

## Can people filter by For sale, Free or Wanted?
Not directly. The filters are by category, not ad type. People find free items and wanted ads by:
- The green **Free** and blue **Wanted** tags on cards.
- The **Free Stuff** and **Wanted / ISO** category pills.
- Sorting by **Price: low to high**, which puts Free ads (price 0) first.
- Searching words like "free" or "ISO" that appear in titles or descriptions.

That's another reason to use the right ad type and, if it helps, a clear word like "Free" or "ISO" in your title.

## How do ad types affect price sorting?
Buyers can sort by **Newest first**, **Price: low to high** or **Price: high to low**.

- Free ads count as $0, so they come first in low to high and last among priced ads in high to low.
- Ads with no price ("Contact for price") and Wanted ads have no number, so they sort to the end in both price orders.

## Can I change the category or ad type after posting?
Yes. Open [My listings](/my/listings), tap **Edit**, change **What are you posting?** or **Category**, and tap **Save changes**. Changes go live straight away and don't change your expiry date. See [Editing, renewing and expiry](/help/editing-renewing-and-expiry).

## Common problems

**My giveaway shows a price instead of "Free".**
You probably picked the **Free Stuff** category but left the ad type on **For sale**. Edit the ad and choose **Free** under **What are you posting?**

**My wanted ad has no Wanted tag.**
The ad type is still **For sale**. Edit it and choose **Wanted**.

**I can't find a Condition box.**
It's hidden for live fish, inverts, coral and plants, and for Wanted ads.

**My ad says "Contact for price" but I wanted Free.**
Leaving the price empty on a For sale ad means "Contact for price". Switch the ad type to **Free**, or enter 0.

**My old ad says "New" on a fish.**
Older ads could carry a condition on livestock. The listing page no longer shows condition for fish, inverts, coral or plants. Editing and saving the ad clears it everywhere.

**I don't see my category's pill on the area page.**
Pills only appear for categories with at least one live ad in that area. If yours is the only ad and it's expired, sold or not yet live, the pill won't show.
