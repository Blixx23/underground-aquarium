Posts are your shop's news: a new shipment, a sale, an event, photos of what just came in. They show on your shop page, newest first, like a business page on a social network. Everyone who follows your shop gets a notification when you post. Only the shop's owner can post.

## How do I post?
You can post from your public page or your dashboard:

1. Open your shop's page (the **Posts** tab opens first), or open **Posts** in your [shop dashboard](/help/shop-dashboard-overview). On a phone you can also tap **+** at the bottom and choose **Post a shop update**.
2. Tap the box that says "Share a restock, sale or new arrival…".
3. Add a headline if you like, for example "Weekend restock".
4. Write what's new.
5. Tap **Add photos** to attach up to 4 pictures.
6. Tap **Post**.

The post appears at the top straight away. To close the box without posting, tap the **X**.

## What can a post include?
| Part | Required? | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | No | Up to 120 characters. It's also the text of the notification your followers see. |
| Text | Text or at least one photo | Write as much as you need. Line breaks are kept. |
| Photos | No | Up to 4 per post. iPhone photos work. |

**Post** stays grayed out until you've written something or added a photo, and while photos are still uploading.

## How do photos in posts look?
One photo shows whole. Two sit side by side. Three show one large and two small. Four show one wide on top and three below. Anyone can tap a photo to see it full screen and swipe through the rest. Every photo you post also collects on your **Photos** tab.

## Who gets notified when I post?
Everyone who follows your shop gets an in-app notification titled "[Shop] posted an update". If your post has a headline, that's the notification text. If not, it says "Tap to see what's new at the shop." Tapping it opens your shop's page.

Followers get the notification once, when you first post. Editing a post later doesn't notify anyone again.

## Where do my posts appear?
- **Your shop page**, on the **Posts** tab.
- **Your dashboard**, on the **Posts** tab.
- **The directory.** The 3 most recent posts from any shop show under **Latest shop updates** on [Fish stores](/stores). Tapping one opens your page.

Posts don't appear in the main community feed.

## How do I edit a post?
1. Tap the pencil on the post.
2. Change the headline or text, or tap the **X** on a photo to take it out.
3. Tap **Save**, or **Cancel**.

You can't add new photos to an existing post. Post a new one instead.

## How do I delete a post?
Tap the trash can on the post and confirm. It's deleted for good.

## What should I post?
- New shipments, with photos of the best livestock
- Sales and clearance
- Events, swaps and club nights at the shop
- Changes like new hours or a move (update **Hours & details** too)

Keep it to real news. Posts follow the [community rules](/help/community-rules).

## Common problems
**"Couldn't post that. Please try again."** The post didn't save. Check your connection and tap **Post** again. Your text and photos stay in the box.

**Post is grayed out.** Write some text or add a photo, and wait for photos to finish uploading.

**I don't see the post box on my page.** Sign in with the account that manages the shop.

**My followers didn't get a notification.** They only get one when you first post, and only if they haven't turned off **Shop updates** in their notification settings.
