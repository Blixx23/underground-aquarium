A thread is a new topic in the [Forums](/forums): a question, a build journal, a photo share or anything else worth talking about. You need to be signed in to start one.

## How do I start a new thread?
1. Sign in.
2. Tap **Ask the forums** on the [Forums](/forums) page, or open the section you want and tap **New post**. On a phone you can also tap the **+** in the bottom bar and choose **Ask the forums**.
3. If you came from **Ask the forums**, pick a **Section** by tapping one of the section buttons. If you came from **New post** inside a section, the thread goes in that section and the page says "New post in" followed by the section name.
4. Type a **Title**.
5. Write the details in **Body**.
6. Optionally add up to 4 **Photos**.
7. Tap **Post**.

You land on your new thread straight away, and it appears in its section and in the feed as "started a discussion".

## What's the difference between Ask the forums and New post?
Both open the same form. **Ask the forums** (at [/forums/new](/forums/new)) lets you choose the section in the form, so you can start from anywhere on the site. **New post** inside a section already knows the section, so there's no section picker. Either way the finished thread is identical.

## Which section should I post in?
Pick the section that best matches your topic: beginner questions in the getting started section, sick fish in fish health, plant questions in plants and aquascaping, and so on. Each section's description on the [Forums](/forums) page explains what belongs there. If you pick the wrong one it's not a big problem, but you can't move the thread yourself. Email support@undergroundaquarium.com if it really needs moving.

## What goes in the Title field?
The **Title** is the headline people see in the thread list, search results and Hot in the forums. The placeholder asks "What's your question or topic?"

- It's required.
- It must be at least 3 characters.
- It can be up to 160 characters. A counter next to the label shows how many you've used, like "42 / 160". It turns amber as you get close and red once you're over, with how many characters over, like "(12 over)". Nothing you type or paste is cut off; you just can't post until it fits.
- You can edit it later with **Edit** (see below), but make it specific from the start. "Cloudy water 3 days after water change in 20 gal" gets far better answers than "Help!!"

The thread's web address is made from the title when you post, so a clear title also makes a clearer link. Editing the title later doesn't change the link, so old links keep working.

## What goes in the Body field?
**Body** is the opening post. The placeholder says "Share the details. Markdown supported." Useful things to include for a question: tank size, how long it's been set up, filter, water test numbers, livestock, what changed recently and what you've tried.

- The body is optional if you add at least one photo. Otherwise you need some text.
- You can format it with Markdown (bold, lists, headings, links and more). See [Formatting your posts](/help/formatting-your-posts).
- The opening post can be up to 20,000 characters. A counter next to the **Body** label shows your count, like "1,234 / 20,000", and turns amber near the limit and red when you're over. Nothing is cut off: if you're over, tapping **Post** tells you how much to shorten it. For an enormous write-up, split it into a thread plus follow-up comments.

## How do I add photos to a thread?
Under **Photos (optional, up to 4)**:

1. Tap the **Add** tile.
2. Choose one or more images. You can pick several at once. On a computer, iPhone .heic files show up in the file picker too.
3. Wait for the thumbnails to appear (the tile shows a spinner while uploading).
4. To remove one, tap the **X** on its thumbnail.

Each photo can be up to 10 MB before upload. iPhone HEIC photos are converted to JPEG automatically, in any browser (the same way as feed photos). Big photos are resized (to at most 2000 pixels on the longest side) and saved as JPEGs, so big phone photos upload quickly. The **Post** button waits until uploads finish.

Photos appear under the opening post, in a grid when there's more than one, and the first photo is used on the thread's Hot in the forums card and when the link is shared. Photos can only be added when you start a thread, and they can't be changed afterward, even when you edit the post. Comments are text only (see [Replying in the forums](/help/replying-in-the-forums)).

## What do the photo messages mean?
- **Up to 4 photos per post.** You tried to add more than 4. The extras were skipped.
- **Images only, please.** One of the files wasn't an image, so it was skipped.
- **That photo is too large (max 10 MB).** Pick a smaller photo, or take a screenshot of it and upload that.
- **A photo failed to upload. Try again.** The upload didn't go through, usually a connection hiccup. Tap **Add** and pick it again.
- **"[file name]" is an iPhone HEIC photo this browser can't read...** Rare, since iPhone photos are normally converted for you. Follow the tip in the message: on your iPhone, **Settings › Camera › Formats › Most Compatible**, or share the photo to yourself first so it comes through as a JPEG.

## What do the error messages mean when I tap Post?
- **Give your post a title (at least 3 characters).** The title is empty or too short.
- **Pick a section for your post.** You started from **Ask the forums** and haven't tapped a section button.
- **Write something, or add a photo.** The body is empty and there are no photos.
- **A title of at least 3 characters is required.** Same as the first message, caught by our server.
- **Add some text or a photo.** Same as "Write something, or add a photo.", caught by our server.
- **The title is [number] characters. The limit is 160, so please shorten it by [number].** Shorten the title by that many characters.
- **Your post is [number] characters. The limit is 20,000, so please shorten it by [number].** Shorten the body by that many characters, or move part of it into a comment.
- **Category not found.** The section you picked no longer exists or isn't public. Reload the page and choose another.
- **Couldn't post.** Something went wrong on our end or with your connection. Your text is still in the form, so wait a moment and tap **Post** again.

## What if I'm not signed in?
The new thread pages send you to sign in first and bring you back to the form afterwards. If your session runs out while you're writing, tapping **Post** takes you to sign in and your **Title** and **Body** are put back in the form when you return (in the same browser tab). Photos aren't saved this way, so you'll need to add them again.

## What happens after I post?
- You're taken to your new thread.
- It shows at the top of the section's **New** tab and near the top of **Active** (pinned threads always stay above it).
- It appears in the [Feed](/feed) as "started a discussion", where people can like it or tap **Reply** to go to the thread.
- It shows on [Unanswered topics](/forums/unanswered) until someone replies.
- If it's your first thread (or first forum post of any kind), you earn bubbles. See [Bubbles](/help/bubbles).

When someone comments on your thread you get a **New reply** notification. See [Replying in the forums](/help/replying-in-the-forums).

## Can I edit, move or delete my thread?
You can edit and delete it, but not move it.

**Editing:** under your opening post, tap **Edit**. You can change the **Title** and the body, with the same limits and live counters as when you posted (title 3 to 160 characters, body up to 20,000). Tap **Save** to keep your changes or **Cancel** to back out. The post then shows a small "(edited)" label next to the time; hover over it on a computer to see exactly when it was edited. The thread's link doesn't change when you edit the title. Photos stay as they are: to change them, delete the thread and post it again. If the thread has photos, the body can be left empty.

**Deleting:** tap **Delete thread** under your opening post and confirm "Delete this whole thread? The opening post and every reply will stop showing. This can't be undone." The whole thread, including everyone's replies, stops showing on the site and you're taken back to the section.

**Locked threads:** once a thread is locked, only our team can edit it. You can still delete it.

**Moving:** you can't move a thread to another section yourself. Email support@undergroundaquarium.com with the link and the section it should be in.

Our team can also edit or delete any thread.

## Tips for getting a good answer
- Put the key facts in the title: species, tank size, the symptom.
- Include water test numbers (ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, temperature) for any health or cloudy-water question. The [Water Check](/water-check) tool can help you read them.
- Add a clear photo for anything visual: disease spots, algae, plants, fish ID.
- Search first. Your question may already have a great answer (see [Searching the forums](/help/searching-the-forums)).

## Common problems
**The Post button does nothing.** Look for a red message at the top of the form. It tells you what's missing (title, section, or text/photo). If photos are still uploading, the button waits until they finish.

**My iPhone photo won't upload.** iPhone HEIC photos are converted automatically, in any browser. If one still fails, the message explains why. On your iPhone, **Settings › Camera › Formats › Most Compatible** makes new photos save as JPEG, or share the photo to yourself first so it comes through as a JPEG, then add it again.

**I picked the wrong section.** You can't move a thread yourself. Email support@undergroundaquarium.com with the link and the section it should be in.

**I posted a typo in the title.** Tap **Edit** under your opening post, fix the title and tap **Save**. The thread's link stays the same. In a locked thread only our team can edit, so email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

**My post is over the limit.** The counter turns red and shows how many characters over you are. Shorten it until the counter is back under the limit, then tap **Post**. Nothing you typed is cut off.

**I lost what I typed.** Drafts are only saved when a sign-in interrupts your post. Before writing something long, make sure you're signed in, or write it somewhere else first and paste it in.
