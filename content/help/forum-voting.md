Every forum thread and comment has a score. Members vote with **Float up** (a bubble icon) and **Sink** (a droplet icon) to push helpful posts up and unhelpful ones down. You need to be signed in to vote.

## Where are the vote buttons?
- On a section's thread list, to the left of each thread title (this votes on the thread's opening post).
- On a thread page, to the left of the opening post.
- On a thread page, to the left of every comment and reply (slightly smaller).

The bubble on top is **Float up**, the number in the middle is the score, and the droplet underneath is **Sink**. Hover over either icon on a computer to see its name.

## How do I vote?
- Tap **Float up** for posts that are helpful, accurate or just great to read.
- Tap **Sink** for posts that are wrong, unhelpful or off-topic.

The score changes instantly. Your vote is shown in color: a filled green bubble means you floated it up, a filled coral droplet means you sank it. The score turns the same color.

## How do I change or take back my vote?
- Tap the same button again to remove your vote. The score goes back to where it was.
- Tap the other button to switch. Going from **Float up** to **Sink** moves the score down by 2 (your +1 is removed and a -1 added), and the reverse moves it up by 2.

You can change your mind as often as you like. Each member has only one vote per post.

## How is the score worked out?
The score is the number of **Float up** votes minus the number of **Sink** votes. It can go below zero. The thread score shown in a section's list is the score of the thread's opening post.

## What do votes affect?
- **The Top tab.** A section's **Top** tab sorts threads by score, highest first.
- **Comment order.** Top-level comments in a thread are shown highest score first, so the best answers rise. Replies under a comment stay in posting order.
- **Hot in the forums.** A thread's positive score adds to how "hot" it is on the home page and feed strip, along with replies and recent activity. Negative scores don't count against it there.
- **The author's bubbles.** Votes from other members count toward the author's bubble balance: **Float up** adds, **Sink** takes away. See [Bubbles](/help/bubbles).
- **Upvote milestones.** Authors earn bonus bubbles for their first upvote received and for reaching 50, 250 and 1,000 upvotes across all their forum posts.
- **Tier-ups.** If a vote pushes the author into a new bubble tier, they get a **New tier** notification and an email. See [Bubble tiers and milestones](/help/bubble-tiers-and-milestones).

Votes don't change the order of the **Active**, **New** or **Unanswered** tabs.

## Can people see how I voted?
No. There's no list of who voted on a post, and the author isn't told who floated or sank their post. Only the total score is shown. You see your own votes highlighted when you're signed in.

## Does the author get a notification when I vote?
Not for the vote itself. The author is only notified if your vote helps them reach a bubble milestone that's announced (for example an upvote milestone), or pushes them into a new tier.

## Can I vote on a locked thread?
Yes. Locking only stops new comments. The vote buttons on the opening post and every comment still work.

## What happens if I vote while signed out?
Tapping **Float up** or **Sink** while signed out takes you to the sign-in page. Your vote isn't saved, so tap it again after you're back on the thread.

## When should I use Sink?
Use **Sink** for content that makes the forum worse: wrong or risky advice, off-topic posts or low-effort replies. Please don't sink posts just because you disagree with someone's taste or approach; reply and explain instead. For spam, harassment, scams or anything against the rules, use **Report** as well, because sinking alone doesn't alert our team. See [Reporting content](/help/reporting-content).

## Common problems
**My vote didn't stick.** If the score jumped back after you tapped, the vote didn't save, usually because of a connection problem or because you were signed out. Refresh the page and try again.

**The buttons don't show my earlier vote right away.** Pages load first and your own votes are filled in a moment later. Give it a second after the page appears.

**A thread's score is different on the list and the thread page.** Section lists refresh about once a minute, so a very recent vote may not show there yet.

**My bubbles went down.** Someone tapped **Sink** on one of your forum posts or tanks, or removed an earlier **Float up**. Your tier won't drop, though. See [Bubble tiers and milestones](/help/bubble-tiers-and-milestones).
