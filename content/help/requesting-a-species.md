If a fish, shrimp, snail or crayfish isn't in the [Fish Species](/species) library, you can ask us to add it. Our team checks the care details by hand. When it's added you get a notification, bubbles and the Cartographer trophy, and the species becomes available in the Tank Builder too.

## How do I request a species?
1. Sign in.
2. Open the request form in one of these ways:
   - At the bottom of [Fish Species](/species), under **Don't see a fish?**, tap **Request a species**.
   - Search [Fish Species](/species) for the fish. If nothing matches, the form opens automatically with your search filled in.
   - In the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder) species search, tap **Request it and earn bubbles** (when nothing matches) or **Not the one? Request "[your search]"** (when there are matches but not the right one).
3. Fill in the form (see the next section).
4. Tap **Send request**.

You'll see **Request sent** and "We'll check its care details and add it. You'll get a notification, bubbles and a trophy when it's in the library."

## What goes in the species request form?
The form has three boxes:

- **Name you know it by** (required): for example "Golden Leopard Dojo Loach". It must be at least 3 characters, up to 80.
- **Scientific name, if you know it** (optional): up to 100 characters. This helps us find the right animal fast.
- **Anything that helps us find it** (optional): where you saw it, what it looks like, a trade name or code. Up to 500 characters.

Under the button you'll see "Bubbles + trophy when it's added" as a reminder. Tap **Cancel** to close the form without sending (the Cancel button doesn't appear when the form opened automatically from an empty search).

## What happens after I request a species?
Our team reviews every request by hand. One of three things happens:

- **It's added.** We create a new species page with its care details. You get a notification, bubbles and the Cartographer trophy, and it shows up in [Fish Species](/species) and the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder) right away.
- **It's another name for a fish we already have.** We add the name you used to that species (so searching it finds the right fish) instead of making a new page. Bubbles are only given when a new species is added.
- **It's turned down.** For example, if it isn't a freshwater species, or the name doesn't match a real animal. The notice tells you why, and you usually get 10 bubbles as a thank-you for helping build the library.

These notifications appear under the **Trophies** filter on your notifications page. See [Notifications](/help/notifications).

## What do I earn for requesting a species?
When a species you requested is added to the library you earn:

- **Bubbles**, once per added request. See [Bubbles](/help/bubbles).
- **The Cartographer trophy** (in the Library & Glossary section of your trophy cabinet). See [Trophies](/help/trophies).

You'll see a "You earned bubbles" notification, and if the bubbles move you into a new tier you'll also get a tier-up notification and email.

## Can I request saltwater fish or plants?
The library is for freshwater aquarium species (fish, shrimp, snails and crayfish, with a few brackish species). Saltwater fish aren't part of it, so a request for one is likely to be turned down. Plants aren't in the species library either.

## Who owns a species I suggest?
The [Terms](/terms#library-submissions) treat species suggestions like other library submissions: once we accept one for the library, it belongs to Underground Aquarium and we can use and change it as we choose. Suggestions we turn down stay yours.

## Can I see or cancel my species requests?
There's no page listing your requests and no way to cancel or edit one after sending. If you made a mistake, send a corrected request with a note, or email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

## Should I check before requesting a species?
Yes, it saves time. Before requesting:

1. On [Fish Species](/species), make sure the group filter is set to **All**.
2. Try the scientific name, other common names, or one distinctive word.
3. For plecos, try the L-number with its leading zero, like **L046**.

See [Searching the Fish Species directory](/help/species-directory) for more search tips.

## Common problems

**The form says "Sign in to request a fish. You earn bubbles and a trophy when it's added."**
You're signed out. Tap **Sign in**, then open the form again.

**"Enter the fish's name."**
The name box is empty or shorter than 3 characters. Type the name you know it by.

**"Couldn't send that. Please try again." or another error.**
Check your connection and try again. If it keeps happening, email support@undergroundaquarium.com with the fish name.

**The Send request button is grayed out.**
The form is still checking that you're signed in, or it's sending. Wait a moment.

**I requested a fish but haven't heard back.**
Requests are reviewed by hand, so it can take a while. You'll get a notification when it's decided.

**I didn't get bubbles for my request.**
Requests that are added earn the full species bubbles. Requests turned down usually earn 10 thank-you bubbles instead (not for spam). If your request turned out to be another name for a species we already had, the notice explains what happened.
