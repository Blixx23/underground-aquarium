Messages are private, one-to-one conversations between you and another member. Chats about a classified ad and direct messages to anyone on the site all live in one inbox at [Messages](/messages). Only the two people in a conversation can read it.

## Where do I find my messages?
- **On a computer:** tap the chat bubble icon in the top bar, next to the notification bell. It only appears when you're signed in.
- **On a phone or in the app:** tap **Messages** in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
- **Anywhere:** go to [Messages](/messages).

If you're signed out, the page says "Sign in to see your messages" with a **Sign in** button.

## What are the two kinds of conversation?
- **Listing conversations** are about a specific classified ad. They start when a buyer taps **Message** on a listing. In your inbox they show the listing's photo and title. You get one conversation per listing per buyer, so if three people ask about your tank, you'll have three separate conversations.
- **Direct messages** are with a person, not about an ad. They start from the **Message** button on someone's profile or from **New message** in your inbox. In your inbox they show the person's profile photo and say "Direct message". You and any other member share just one direct conversation, whoever started it.

The same two people can have both: one direct conversation plus a separate conversation for each listing they've talked about. See [Starting a conversation](/help/starting-a-conversation).

## What's in my inbox?
[Messages](/messages) lists your conversations, most recent activity first. Each row shows:

- a photo: the listing's cover photo for listing conversations, or the person's profile photo for direct messages,
- the other person's name,
- how long ago the last message was sent,
- the listing title, or "Direct message", or "Listing removed" if the ad was deleted,
- a preview of the latest message, starting with "You:" if you sent it ("📷 Photo" if it was just a picture),
- a status tag: **New**, **Sent** or **Seen**.

At the top, a pill shows how many conversations are unread (for example "2 unread"). The **New message** button is on the right.

The inbox shows 30 conversations at a time. Tap **Show older conversations** at the bottom to load more. It updates by itself: when a new message arrives, that conversation jumps to the top and gets a **New** tag without you refreshing.

## How do I find an old conversation?
Once you have more than a handful of conversations, a **Search by name or listing** box appears above the list. Type part of the person's name, their @username or the ad title and press Enter. Clear the box to see everything again.

## What do Unread, New, Sent and Seen mean?
- **Unread:** conversations where the other person sent a message you haven't opened yet. They're grouped at the top under **Unread**, highlighted with a green edge, in bold, with a **New** tag.
- **Read:** everything else, listed under **Read** below the unread ones (the heading only appears when you have unread conversations).
- **Sent:** you sent the last message and the other person hasn't opened the conversation since.
- **Seen:** the other person has opened the conversation since your last message.

**Sent** and **Seen** also show under your last message inside the conversation, and change from Sent to Seen while you watch.

## Reading a conversation
Tap any row in your inbox to open it. At the top is a box showing what the conversation is about:

- **Listing conversations:** "With" and the other person's name, the listing title (tap it to open the ad) and its price. If the ad sold, the price line adds "sold". If it's off the market for another reason, it says "no longer listed". If the seller deleted the ad, it says "Listing removed". Wanted ads don't show a price.
- **Direct messages:** "Direct message", the person's name (tap it to open their profile) and their @username.

Below that, messages run oldest at the top to newest at the bottom, in their own scrolling box. Yours are on the right and theirs on the left. The conversation opens at the newest message, and the reply box stays at the bottom of the screen.

New messages appear by themselves while the conversation is open. You don't need to refresh.

Tap **All messages** at the top to go back to your inbox. On a phone, the bottom bar is hidden inside a conversation to give you more room.

## How do I reply?
1. Open the conversation.
2. Type in the box at the bottom. It says "Reply to" followed by the person's name.
3. Tap **Send**, or press **Enter**.

Press **Shift + Enter** for a new line without sending. Messages can be up to 4,000 characters.

## What are the quick reply buttons?
In listing conversations, a row of one-tap starters sits above the reply box when it's empty. Tapping one fills in the box so you can change it before sending. Nothing sends until you tap **Send**.

- **Buyers see:** "Is this still available?", "What's your best price?", "Can you send more photos?" and "When could I pick it up?"
- **Sellers see:** "Yes, it's still available!", "Sorry, it's sold.", "When works for you to pick up?" and "The price is firm."

## Can I send photos?
Yes.
1. Tap **Photo** next to the Send button.
2. Pick up to 4 photos. iPhone photos (HEIC) are converted for you.
3. Add a message if you like, or send the photos on their own.
4. Tap **Send**.

Tap the **X** on a preview to remove it before sending. In the conversation, tap any photo to see it full screen, and swipe if there's more than one.

Photos you send are stored like listing photos: anyone who has a photo's exact web address could open it, so don't send pictures of anything private, like ID or bank details.

## Can I send links?
You can type a web address, but it won't turn into a clickable link, so the other person would need to copy it.

## Can I mark my ad sold from the conversation?
Yes. If you're the seller and the ad is still active, a green **Mark as sold** button (**Mark as found** for Wanted ads) sits under the listing title at the top of the conversation. See [Marking sold and deleting](/help/marking-sold-and-deleting).

## Can I archive a conversation?
Yes. Archiving hides a finished conversation from your inbox without deleting it.

1. Open the conversation.
2. Tap **Archive conversation** under the reply box.

Archived conversations are listed under **Archived** at the top of your inbox. If the other person sends a new message, the conversation comes back to your inbox by itself. To bring one back sooner, open it from **Archived** and tap **Move back to inbox**.

Archiving only changes your inbox. The other person still sees the conversation as normal.

## Can I edit or delete a message?
No. Once a message is sent, it can't be edited or deleted, and conversations can't be deleted. If you sent something by mistake, send a follow-up message to correct it.

If a conversation contains something that needs to be removed (for example harassment or personal information), email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

## What's the yellow "Tip" under some messages?
If the other person mentions payment apps like Zelle, Cash App or Venmo, gift cards, a deposit or shipping before you've met, a small yellow tip appears under their message: paying in person at pickup is safest. It's only a reminder. Plenty of honest hobbyists use those apps, and nothing is blocked. See [Blocking and safety in messages](/help/blocking-and-safety-in-messages).

## Who can read my messages?
Only you and the other person in the conversation can open it. Anyone else who tries to open the link gets a "page not found" message. Your email address is never shown in messages. The other person sees your name and username.

Messages are included when you download a copy of your data from [Account & data](/account). See [Privacy and your data](/help/privacy-and-your-data).

## Common problems
**I can't find a conversation.**
Use the search box above your inbox, tap **Show older conversations** at the bottom, and check **Archived**. Sending a new message in an old conversation moves it back to the top.

**The unread count won't go away.**
Open each conversation under **Unread** in your inbox. The badge updates within about a minute, or right away when you switch back to the tab.

**A photo won't send.**
Check your connection and try again. If one photo can't be read, you'll see "One of those photos couldn't be read. Try a different one." See [Messages troubleshooting](/help/messages-troubleshooting).

**The other person's name looks different in different places.**
Your inbox shows people's display name when they have one. Inside a listing conversation, and in the emails we send, you may see their username instead. It's the same person.

**It says "Sign in to read this conversation."**
You're signed out. Sign in with the account that's part of the conversation.

More fixes in [Messages troubleshooting](/help/messages-troubleshooting).
