Saving an ad keeps it in one list so you can come back to it, and we'll tell you if the price drops or it sells. Only you can see what you've saved, and the seller isn't told.

## How do I save an ad?
1. Open the ad.
2. Tap **Save this ad** (the heart), under the seller's contact buttons.

The button turns red and says **Saved**, with a note: "We'll let you know if the price drops or it sells." Tap it again to unsave.

The button only shows on live ads. You need to be signed in. If you're signed out, tapping **Save this ad** takes you to sign in and then back to the ad. You won't see the button on your own ads.

## Where are my saved ads?
Go to [Saved ads](/my/saved). You can also get there from:

- **Saved ads** in your account menu (your photo at the top right on a computer),
- **Saved ads** on your [Dashboard](/profile) (the **Me** tab on a phone),
- the **Your saved ads** link that appears under the heart after you save something.

Saved ads are shown newest saved first, with the photo, title and price. Tap one to open it.

## What happens when a saved ad sells or comes down?
It stays in your list, faded, with a tag:

- **Sold:** the seller marked it sold. Opening it shows a short Sold page with buttons to see what's listed nearby or set an alert.
- **No longer listed:** it came off the classifieds another way.

Sold ads stay in your list so you can see what happened to them. If the seller deletes the ad, it drops out of your list.

## What notifications do saved ads send?
- **Price drop:** "Price drop: (title)", with the new price and what it was before. You're told each time it goes lower than the last price you were told about.
- **Sold:** "Sold: (title)", once, with a nudge to set an alert so you hear when another one is posted.

These arrive in your notification bell, by email (unless you've turned it off) and on your phone if you use the [iPhone app](/help/using-the-site-on-your-phone). Updates are checked once an hour, so they can take up to an hour to arrive.

To stop them, turn off **Saved ads** in your [notification settings](/notifications#settings). Your saved list keeps working either way.

## Saving an ad vs. setting an alert
- **Save** an ad when you're interested in that one ad.
- **Set an alert** when you want to hear about new ads for something, like "discus" near you. See [Classified alerts](/help/classified-alerts).

## Common problems
**I don't see Save this ad.**
It's on other people's ads, not your own. If you're on your own ad, you'll see **Mark as sold** and **Manage this listing** instead.

**The heart flipped back after I tapped it.**
The save didn't go through, usually because your connection dropped. Tap it again.

**I didn't get a price drop notification.**
Check **Saved ads** is on in your [notification settings](/notifications#settings), and give it up to an hour. A price that goes up and then back down to where it was before isn't a drop.
