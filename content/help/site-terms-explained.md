Underground Aquarium uses a few words of its own. This page explains what they mean on the site. For fish keeping terms like "cycling" or "bioload", use the [Glossary](/glossary) instead (see [Glossary](/help/glossary)).

## Account and community words

### Username and @handle
Your public name, made of letters, numbers and underscores, shown as @yourname. Your public profile lives at /u/yourname. See [Choosing a username and the Welcome page](/help/choosing-a-username-and-welcome).

### Dashboard
Your private home page at [/profile](/profile), called **Dashboard & settings** in the menu and opened by **Me** on phones. Only you see it. Your **public profile** is the page everyone else sees.

### Feed
The stream of posts, photos, tank updates, new listings and milestones at [/feed](/feed). It has two tabs: **Following** (people and shops you follow) and **Everyone**. See [The feed](/help/the-feed).

### Following
Choosing to see someone's posts in your **Following** feed. You can follow members and shops. See [Following people and shops](/help/following-people-and-shops).

### Tanks
Your aquariums, shown on your profile with photos and stocking. You can build them in the Tank Builder and share them. See [Sharing your tank](/help/sharing-your-tank).

## Bubbles and bubble tiers
**Bubbles** are points you earn for taking part: posting, helping, sharing and coming back. They add up to a balance shown on your Dashboard and profile. They're a reputation score, not money.

Your balance places you in a **bubble tier**. There are 21 tiers named after ocean depths, starting at **Tide Pool** (0 bubbles), then **Shallows** (25), **Kelp Forest** (75) and on down to **Leviathan** (125,000), the hardest to reach. Reaching a new tier sends you a notification. See [Bubbles](/help/bubbles) and [Bubble tiers and milestones](/help/bubble-tiers-and-milestones).

The bubbles floating up behind every page are only decoration.

## Trophies
Badges you earn automatically for things you do on the site, like posting, helping others, sharing tanks and inviting friends. The site describes 140 of them, and they show in a trophy case on your public profile. The Society has its own set of trophies for members. See [Trophies](/help/trophies).

## Classifieds words

### Classifieds, ad and listing
Free local ads for fish, shrimp, snails, plants, coral, tanks and gear. "Ad" and "listing" mean the same thing. There's no fee, no commission and no checkout: buyer and seller message on the site and arrange the rest themselves. See [Posting a classified ad](/help/posting-a-classified-ad).

### State and area
Ads are organized by state, then by **area**, which is a metro area inside that state (for example Sacramento in California). You pick your area when you post, so your ad shows to keepers near you. "Browse by area" in the footer opens the list of states and areas. See [Choosing your area](/help/choosing-your-area).

### Wanted / ISO
A category for ads where you're looking for something instead of selling it. ISO means "in search of". Wanted ads show "Wanted" instead of a price, and you mark them **Mark found** instead of **Mark sold**.

### Free Stuff
A category for things you're giving away. The **Free Stuff** pill also shows every ad posted as **Free**, whatever its category, and the **Wanted / ISO** pill shows every wanted ad. See [Searching and filtering listings](/help/searching-and-filtering-listings).

### Bumped and renewing
Ads last 45 days. **Renew 45 days** on [My listings](/my/listings) gives an ad a fresh 45 days and moves it back to the top as if it were just posted. "Bumped" shows when an ad was last posted or renewed. See [Editing, renewing and expiry](/help/editing-renewing-and-expiry).

### Expired, sold, draft and removed
Listing statuses you may see on My listings: **active** (live), **draft** (not published yet), **sold** (marked sold or found), **expired** (its 45 days ran out) and **removed** (taken down). See [My listings](/help/my-listings).

## Forum words

### Thread and reply
A **thread** is a forum topic someone started. Everyone else adds **replies** under it. See [Forums overview](/help/forums-overview).

### Float up and Sink
Our words for upvote and downvote. **Float up** means a post is helpful. **Sink** means it isn't. See [Forum voting](/help/forum-voting).

### Hot, Unanswered, pinned and locked
**Hot in the forums** lists busy threads. **Unanswered topics** are threads still waiting for a reply (see [Unanswered threads](/help/unanswered-threads)). A **pinned** thread stays at the top of its category. A **locked** thread says "This thread is locked." and can't get new replies, and only our team can edit posts in it.

### Edited
A forum post marked "(edited)" was changed by its author or our team after it was posted. Hover over the label on a computer to see when.

## The Society words

### The Society
Short for the **Underground Aquarium Society**, our national membership club for serious keepers: one national body, no chapters. It has membership dues. Members log spawns, earn titles and get signed certificates. See [The Society overview](/help/the-society-overview).

### Member number
Every Society member gets a permanent number in the format UAS-0001, shown in the member area and on their Dashboard.

### Good standing
A member whose membership is current. Members in good standing wear a **gold ring** around their profile photo across the site.

### Dues, lifetime and honorary lifetime
**Dues** are the Society's yearly membership fee. Renewal opens 30 days before your paid-through date, and renewing early adds a full year on top, so you don't lose any days. **Lifetime membership** is a one-time payment instead of yearly dues. **Honorary lifetime** membership is granted free by the Society. See [Society dues and renewal](/help/society-dues-and-renewal) and [Lifetime and honorary membership](/help/lifetime-and-honorary-membership).

### Member area
The members-only part of The Society, with spawn logs, the breeder program, certificates and the leaderboard. See [Society member area](/help/society-member-area).

### Breeder Award Program
The Society's program that awards points and titles for spawning and raising fish. People often call it the BAP. See [Breeder Award Program](/help/breeder-award-program).

### Spawn log
A dated record of one breeding attempt, built in stages with photos, that you submit for judging. Its status moves through **In progress**, **Submitted**, **Peer review**, **With the judge**, and then **Approved** or **Rejected**. It can also be **Under appeal** or **Withdrawn**. See [Spawn logs and submissions](/help/spawn-logs-and-submissions).

### Challenge code
A unique code issued when you open a spawn log. You write it on a card and put it beside the pair in your first photo, which proves the photos are new and yours.

### Peer review and the judge
Other members review a submitted spawn log first (**Peer review**), then a Society judge makes the final decision (**With the judge**).

### Points and classes
Each species is worth points based on how hard it is to breed, from class **A** (5 points, breeds readily in a community tank) through **B** (10), **C** (15), **D** (20), **E** (25) to **F** (40, rarely bred in captivity at all).

### First in the Society
A label on an approved spawn when it's the first time any member has bred that species in the Society.

### Certificate and Certificate Registry
Society members get a signed certificate for each title they earn. Anyone can check a certificate is real on the **Certificate Registry** at [Verify a certificate](/verify). See [Society certificates](/help/society-certificates) and [Verifying a certificate](/help/verifying-a-certificate).

### Leaderboard
The ranking of Society members by breeder points. See [Society leaderboard](/help/society-leaderboard).

### Officer
A member who helps run a club or the Society and can see club management pages. See [Clubs](/help/clubs).

## Fish store words

### Independent fish store
The store directory lists independent aquarium shops, not big chains. **Shops Near Me** sorts them by distance from you. See [Finding a fish store](/help/finding-a-fish-store).

### Claimed shop
A store page the real owner has taken over. Claimed shops show a check badge ("Claimed by the owner") and can post updates, set hours, add photos and reply to reviews. Owners can claim their shop for free. See [Claiming your store](/help/claiming-your-store).

### Suggest a fix
The link on a store page for reporting wrong details, like hours or an address.

## Tools words

### Tank Builder
A planner at [/tank-builder](/tank-builder) where you pick a tank and stock it, and it warns you about aggression, size and bioload problems before you buy. See [Tank Builder](/help/tank-builder).

### Water Check
A tool at [/water-check](/water-check) where you enter your water test results and get a plain answer about what's wrong. See [Water Check](/help/water-check).

### Trade code
A code the hobby uses for fish without a settled name, like "L046" for a plecostomus. You can search Fish Species by trade code. See [Species directory](/help/species-directory).

## Common problems

**I earned bubbles. Can I spend them?**
No. Bubbles are a reputation score that sets your tier. There's nothing to spend them on, and they have no cash value.

**I see a gold ring around some people's photos. What does it mean?**
They're members of the Underground Aquarium Society in good standing.

**What's the difference between my Dashboard and my profile?**
Your Dashboard ([/profile](/profile)) is private and holds your settings and shortcuts. Your public profile (/u/yourname) is what other people see.

**Why does my ad say "Bumped 3 days ago" when I posted it weeks ago?**
You renewed it, which counts as a fresh posting and moves it back to the top.

**I don't see a word here.**
Try the [Glossary](/glossary) for fish keeping terms, or search the [Help Center](/help). If it's a word on our site that isn't explained anywhere, email support@undergroundaquarium.com and we'll add it.
