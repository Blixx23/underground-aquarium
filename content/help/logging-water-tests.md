You can keep a history of your water tests by logging them to a saved tank. Each reading is stamped with the date and time you log it, can carry a short note, and appears under **Recent readings** for that tank in the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder), with **Trends** lines once you have a few. Logging needs an account and a saved tank.

## Where do I log a water test?
There are two places, and both save to the same tank history:

- The **Water test** tab of the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder) (or the built-in Tank Builder on a [tank size guide](/help/tank-size-guides) page).
- [Water Check](/water-check), using **Check against one of your tanks** (see below).

You need to be signed in. If you're not, the **Water test** tab shows "Sign in to log readings and track your water over time." Tap **Sign in**; you'll come back to the Tank Builder afterward. On Water Check, nothing is saved unless you're signed in and pick one of your tanks.

## How do I log a reading to a saved tank?
1. Open the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder) and tap your tank under **Your tanks** to load it.
2. Switch the results panel to the **Water test** tab.
3. Enter your results under **Enter your test results** (Temperature, pH, Ammonia, Nitrite, Nitrate, GH, KH). Fill in only what you tested.
4. Under **Log this reading** ("Adds a dated entry to "[tank name]" so you can watch changes over time."), add an optional note.
5. Tap **Log reading**.

You'll see "Reading logged." The note box clears and the reading appears at the top of **Recent readings**. The values you typed stay in the boxes, so you can compare with your fish's needs; clear them yourself before your next test.

## How do I log a reading from Water Check?
1. Sign in and open [Water Check](/water-check).
2. Enter your results under **Your reading**.
3. In the **Check against one of your tanks** box, pick a tank from the list. The check now also compares your numbers with the fish in that tank (or says "This tank has no fish yet, so only the general checks apply").
4. Add an optional note (up to 200 characters).
5. Tap **Log reading to this tank**.

You'll see **Logged. See the history in the Tank Builder.** Tap it to open that tank in the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder), where the reading is at the top of **Recent readings**. The note box clears; your values stay in the boxes.

The list includes all your saved tanks, public and private. If you're signed in but haven't saved a tank yet, Water Check shows a link to the Tank Builder instead: "Save a tank in the Tank Builder to check readings against your fish and keep a history." Water Check can't save a new tank for you.

## Can I log a reading without saving a tank first?
Yes, in one step. If you haven't saved or loaded a tank, the box is titled **Track this over time** and says "Readings attach to a saved tank. This saves your build and logs the reading in one step."

1. Enter your results (and an optional note).
2. Tap **Save tank & log reading**.

In the Tank Builder, yes. The builder saves your current build as a new tank (using the name in the save box, or a name based on its size, or "My Tank") and then logs the reading to it. This counts toward your 4 saved tank limit. If you already have 4 tanks, nothing is saved or logged, and the message "You've saved 4 tanks" appears in the save section under **3. Save your build** (scroll up to see it on a phone). Load an existing tank first, or delete one. See [Saving and sharing tanks](/help/saving-and-sharing-tanks).

## What can I record in a reading?
Each reading can include any of these, all optional, but at least one value is required:

- **Temperature** (°F)
- **pH**
- **Ammonia** (ppm)
- **Nitrite** (ppm)
- **Nitrate** (ppm)
- **GH** (dGH)
- **KH** (dKH)
- **Note**: free text up to 200 characters (placeholder "Optional note (e.g. after a 30% water change)")

Temperature must be in Fahrenheit; convert Celsius first (multiply by 9, divide by 5, add 32).

The log button stays grayed out until you've entered at least one value. If you somehow try with an empty form you'll see "Enter at least one value first."

## When is a reading dated?
A reading is stamped with the date and time at the moment you tap **Log reading**. You can't choose a different date or time, so log right after you test. If you're catching up on old tests, add the real test date in the note (for example "tested Sunday").

Dates show in your device's local format, for example "Jun 16, 2026 · 3:40 PM."

## Where do I see my past readings?
1. Load the saved tank in the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder).
2. Switch to the **Water test** tab.
3. Scroll to **Recent readings**.

Each entry shows the date and time, a one-line summary of the values you recorded (for example "Temp 78°F · pH 7.2 · Ammonia 0 ppm · Nitrate 10 ppm"), and your note in italics if you added one. Values you left blank aren't shown.

**Recent readings** appears only when a saved tank is loaded and it has at least one reading. Each saved tank has its own separate history; readings never move between tanks. Readings you logged from Water Check show here too.

## How many readings are kept?
**Recent readings** shows the **latest 20** readings for that tank, newest first. Older readings aren't deleted; they just aren't shown in the builder. To see every reading you've ever logged, use **Download my data** on your [Account & data](/account) page, which includes all your tanks and water readings. See [Privacy and your data](/help/privacy-and-your-data).

There's no limit on how many readings you can log.

## What do the Trends lines show?
Once a saved tank has **2 or more** logged tests, the **Water test** tab shows a **Trends** box above **Recent readings** ("Your last [number] tests, oldest on the left."). It has one small line for each value you've recorded at least twice: nitrate, ammonia, nitrite, pH, temperature, GH and KH, in that order.

- Each line covers up to the **last 12 tests** that include that value, oldest on the left and newest on the right. It's the quickest way to spot a slow climb, like nitrate creeping up between water changes.
- Next to each line is your latest value. The line and number are colored by how that latest reading grades: **green** healthy, **blue** heads-up, **amber** to watch, **red** needs action. This uses the general ranges from [Water test results explained](/help/water-check-readings-explained), not your fish's ranges.
- On the right it says how the latest value compares with the test before it: "up [amount] since last test," "down [amount] since last test" or "same as last test."

A value you've only recorded once has no line yet. Each line is scaled to its own readings, so a small change can look steep; check the numbers before you worry.

## How do I delete a reading?
1. In **Recent readings**, tap the trash icon on the right of the entry.
2. Confirm "Delete this reading?"

The reading is removed permanently. If it fails you'll see "Couldn't delete that reading." Try again.

## Can I edit a reading?
No. Readings can't be edited after you log them. To fix a mistake, delete the wrong reading and log a corrected one. Note that the corrected one will carry the new date and time.

## Does logging water tests earn bubbles?
Yes. Logging your **first ever** water reading earns bubbles once. Logging at least one reading in a week also earns bubbles, once per week (weeks run Monday to Sunday, in UTC time). Readings logged from the Tank Builder and from Water Check count the same. See [Bubbles](/help/bubbles).

Logging many readings in the same week doesn't earn extra weekly bubbles.

## Can other people see my water readings?
No. Water readings are only shown to you, in the Tank Builder, when that tank is loaded. They don't appear on a public tank page, your profile or the feed, even if the tank is public.

## What happens to readings if I delete a tank?
They're deleted with it. The delete confirmation says "Delete "[name]"? Its water test history goes with it." Download your data first from [Account & data](/account) if you want to keep them.

## What does the Water test tab tell me about my reading?
As you type, the tab grades your numbers (red, amber, heads-up or green) with a banner: "Something needs attention now," "A few things to keep an eye on," or "Your water looks healthy." Each result is a card with **How to fix** advice. Because the Tank Builder knows your fish, it also checks whether your pH, temperature and GH suit them. Water Check does the same fish checks when you pick a tank. See [Water test results explained](/help/water-check-readings-explained).

The grading happens whether or not you log the reading; logging just saves the numbers.

## Common problems
**I don't see a log button.**
Sign in. Signed out, the tab only shows a **Sign in** link. On Water Check, the **Log reading to this tank** button only appears after you pick a tank in **Check against one of your tanks**.

**Water Check doesn't show "Check against one of your tanks."**
You need to be signed in and have at least one saved tank. Save one in the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder) first.

**The log button is grayed out.**
Enter at least one value. It's also briefly disabled while a save is in progress.

**I tapped Save tank & log reading and nothing happened.**
You're probably at the 4 tank limit. Scroll up to the save section to see the message. Load one of **Your tanks** and log to it instead, or delete a tank to make room.

**I logged the same reading twice.**
Your values stay in the boxes after logging (in both places), so tapping again logs a duplicate. Delete the extra with the trash icon.

**My old readings are missing.**
Only the latest 20 per tank show. Make sure you've loaded the right tank. Use **Download my data** on [Account & data](/account) to see all of them.

**My reading shows the wrong time.**
Readings are stamped when you tap **Log reading**, in your device's time zone. They can't be backdated; add the real test time in the note.

**I see "Couldn't log the reading. Please try again."**
A connection or server problem. Check your connection and tap **Log reading** again. If it keeps failing, email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

**Readings from one tank don't show on another.**
Each saved tank keeps its own history. Load the tank you logged to.

**I don't see Trends.**
Trends need a saved tank with at least 2 logged tests, and a value only gets a line once it's been recorded in at least 2 of them.
