The **Shop Owners Lounge** is a forum only for people who manage a shop on Underground Aquarium. Use it to trade tips with other shop owners, ask about the site, and share what's working for you.

## Where do I find it?
Open [Forums](/forums). Once you manage a shop, the Lounge sits at the top of the page with a green glow and "Only shop owners can see this" under its name. Tap it to open it.

## Who can see it?
Only people who manage a claimed shop, plus the Underground Aquarium team. Everyone else, including signed-out visitors and search engines, can't see it at all. A direct link to a Lounge thread shows them "not found."

## Does anything I post there show up elsewhere?
No. Lounge threads never appear in the Feed, site search, the forum search, the unanswered list, other members' profiles or Google.

## How do I start a thread?
Open the Lounge and tap the button to start a new post. It works just like the rest of the forums: a title, your text and up to 4 photos. Replies, votes and editing work the same way too.

## Common problems
**I run a shop but don't see the Lounge.** You need to have claimed your shop, and the claim must be approved. Check [My shops](/my/shops). If your shop is there and you still don't see it, sign out and back in, then email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

**Someone shared a Lounge link and it says not found.** Only shop owners can open Lounge threads.
