Every shop in the directory has its own page at an address like /stores/shop-name. Anyone can read it without signing in. It's laid out like a business page on a social network: a banner across the top, the shop's logo and name, a row of buttons, and tabs underneath.

## What's at the top of a store page?
- **A back link.** "More fish stores in [city]" takes you to that city's page. Shops with no city show "All fish stores" instead.
- **The banner.** A wide photo chosen by the shop, often the storefront or a display tank. Shops that haven't added one show a plain ocean banner with their name.
- **The logo.** The round picture next to the name. Shops without a logo show their initials.
- **The shop name**, the town, and the **stars**: the average rating out of 5 with the number of reviews. Tap the stars to open the Reviews tab. Five dim stars mean no reviews yet.
- **Owner managed**, with a green check, if someone from the shop has claimed the page.
- **Specialty tags**, such as saltwater, plants or pond.

## What do the buttons under the name do?
- **Directions** opens Google Maps in a new tab with the shop's address (or its name and town if we have no street address).
- **Call** dials the shop on a phone. It only shows when we have a number.
- **Website** opens the shop's own site in a new tab.
- **The heart** follows the shop, and the number beside it is how many people follow it. Following gets you a notification when the shop posts. See [Favoriting and following stores](/help/favoriting-and-following-stores).

## What are the tabs?
- **Posts** (the page opens here): the shop's own posts, newest first, with an **Intro** box beside them.
- **Photos**: every photo the shop has shared, in one gallery.
- **Reviews**: what customers say, with the owner's replies.
- **About**: the full description, contact details, hours, special hours, the map and nearby shops.

## What's on the Posts tab?
The shop's news: new shipments, sales, events, photos of new arrivals. Only the shop posts here. Each post shows the shop's logo and name, the date, an optional headline, the text and any photos. Tap a photo to see it full screen, then swipe or use the arrows to see the rest.

Some posts say "[Shop] added 8 photos". Those are photos the shop added to its gallery, grouped by the day they were added.

Beside the posts (above them on a phone) you'll find:

- **Intro**: the shop's description, address, phone, website and hours.
- **Special hours**, if any are coming up.
- **Photos**: a small preview of the shop's latest photos, with **See all**.
- **Reviews**: the average rating, with a link to read or write one.

If the shop hasn't posted yet, the tab says so. Unclaimed shops can't post until someone from the shop claims the page.

## How do I see what a shop has in stock?
Look at the shop's latest posts. Shops that manage their page post restocks and new arrivals with photos. Stock at small shops changes fast, so if you're after something specific, tap **Call** before you drive over.

## How do I check a store's hours?
Regular hours are in the **Intro** box on the Posts tab, and on the About tab. They're written as plain text, for example "Mon-Sat 11am-7pm, Sun 12-5", so read them the way the shop wrote them.

If hours aren't listed, **Good to know** says "Hours aren't listed yet. Call [number] before you go." (or "Check with the shop before you go." when there's no phone number).

## Does the page show if a store is open now?
No. The site doesn't work out whether a shop is open or closed at this moment. It shows the regular hours as written plus any **Special hours** coming up.

## What are Special hours?
Shops that manage their page can add holiday closures and one-off hours. Upcoming ones show under **Special hours**, one line per date, for example:

- **Thu, Nov 27** Closed · Thanksgiving
- **Sat, Dec 20** Open 10am to 2pm

Only today and future dates are shown, and each one disappears once the day has passed. If the shop has none coming up, the box isn't shown.

## What does Owner managed mean?
**Owner managed** (with a green check) means someone from the shop claimed the page and our team approved it. They keep their hours and details current, post updates and photos, and reply to reviews. On a claimed page, the only thing customers add is reviews.

The same green check appears on the shop's card in the directory.

## What does it mean if a store isn't claimed?
An unclaimed page was built from public listings, so it may only have a name, a town and sometimes a phone number. Nobody from the shop is maintaining it yet. You'll see **Own this store?** with a **Claim it** button. If you run the shop, see [Claiming your store](/help/claiming-your-store).

On unclaimed pages you can still leave a review, follow the shop, and use **Something wrong here? Suggest a fix** if the details are wrong. See [Suggesting store fixes](/help/suggesting-store-fixes-and-sightings).

## How do reviews and ratings work on a store page?
Open the **Reviews** tab. It shows the count and the average rating. Each review shows the reviewer's name, their stars, the date ("· Edited" if they changed it) and what they wrote. If the owner replied, their answer appears under the review as **Owner's response**. Reviews are listed newest first. For writing, editing and deleting reviews, see [Writing a store review](/help/writing-a-store-review).

## What's in Good to know?
**Good to know about [shop]** is at the bottom of the Posts tab and on the About tab. It answers the questions people usually ask:

- **Where is it?** The full address, with a **Get directions** link.
- **What are its hours?** The listed hours, or a note that they aren't listed yet.
- **What does it sell?** Shown when the shop's tags include saltwater, freshwater, plants, shrimp or pond.
- **What other aquarium stores are near it?** Up to 4 shops within 30 miles, with distances.

## Where is the map?
On the **About** tab, under **On the map**. The shop is the gold pin and nearby shops are green pins. It only appears when we know the shop's location. **Other fish stores nearby** lists up to 4 other shops within 30 miles.

## What changes when I'm signed in?
Signed in, you can follow the shop with the heart, leave or edit your review, use **Claim it**, and on unclaimed pages send a **Suggest a fix** report. Signed out, these ask you to sign in first.

If you manage the shop, you'll also see a **Shop dashboard** button, **Add a banner** (or **Change banner**) on the banner, a camera button on your logo, a post box at the top of the Posts tab, a **Respond** link on reviews, and edit and delete buttons on your own posts.

## What is the OpenStreetMap note?
Some shop listings were imported from OpenStreetMap, a free public map. Those pages carry a small credit line saying where the location came from.

## Common problems
**The hours are wrong or the shop has closed.** On an unclaimed page, tap **Something wrong here? Suggest a fix**. On a claimed page, the owner keeps the details, so call the shop or email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

**Get directions goes to the wrong spot.** Directions search by the address on the page. If the address is wrong, see the step above.

**The page says "Page not found".** The shop may have been removed from the directory, or the address was mistyped. Search for it on [Fish stores](/stores).

**The Posts or Photos tab is empty.** The shop hasn't shared anything yet.
