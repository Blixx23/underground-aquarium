The Notify members screen at /admin/notify sends one notification to every member's bell at once. It shows up as **Site news** under the bell, with whatever title, message and link you give it.

## Where is it?
Pick **Notify members** in the Admin side menu under Community (subtitle "Send everyone a notification").

## Who gets it?
Every member except:
- suspended or deleted accounts
- members who switched **Site news** off under the bell

The green button shows how many members it will reach, for example **Send to 412 members**. You always get a copy yourself.

## Does it email people too?
Yes, unless you untick **Also email members who get Site news by email**. The normal notification email job sends it about 10 minutes later, only to members who haven't read it on the site yet, and only if they get Site news by email. Members on the once-a-day setting get it in their morning email. Untick the box for bell only.

## How do I send one?
1. Write a **Title** (up to 80 characters) and a **Message** (up to 300).
2. Under **When they tap it, take them to**, pick a page. **Other page** takes any site address starting with /. Outside links aren't allowed.
3. Check **How it looks in the bell**.
4. Press **Send a test to me** and look at your bell if you want to see the real thing first.
5. Press **Send to N members**, then **Yes, send it to everyone**.

A green message confirms how many members it went to. A send can't be taken back, and the same message can't go out twice by double clicking.

## The Sent list
Everything you've sent, newest first, with how many members it reached, whether it was bell only or bell and email, and the link. Tests show as **Test to you**.
