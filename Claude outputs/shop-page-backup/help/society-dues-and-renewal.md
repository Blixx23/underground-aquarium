Society dues are the only paid feature on Underground Aquarium. You pay them on the membership page through a secure Stripe checkout. Nothing is ever charged automatically: each year you choose to pay again.

## How much are Society dues?
Current prices are shown on the site, not in this guide:

- The **What it costs** section of [the Society page](/society#dues).
- The plan cards on [the membership page](/c/underground-aquarium-society).
- The **Pay** button itself, which always shows the exact amount you'll be charged.

**Individual** dues are "per year." **Lifetime** dues are "once · never renews." Family memberships are no longer offered. The site takes no cut of dues.

## How do I pay my Society dues?
You can only pay after your application has been approved.

1. Sign in and go to [the membership page](/c/underground-aquarium-society). (Society links also take you there automatically when dues are owed.)
2. Find the payment box. Its title depends on where you are:
   - **Activate your membership** for your first payment, with the button **Pay [amount] dues**
   - **Your membership expires in [number] days** (or **Your membership expires today**) when you're renewing in the last 30 days, with the button **Renew for [amount]**
   - **Renew your membership** after your membership has lapsed, with the button **Renew for [amount]**
   - **Lifetime membership** on the Lifetime plan, with the button **Pay [amount]**
3. For yearly plans, the box also shows "Paid through after this payment:" and the exact date your payment will cover you to. Click the button. It shows "Starting..." while it opens checkout.
4. Complete the payment on the Stripe checkout page. Your email address is filled in for you.
5. You come back to the membership page with a green banner: "Dues paid, thank you! Your membership is active." The banner disappears after a few seconds.
6. Click **Go to the member area**.

## How long does one Society payment last?
- **Individual:** each payment covers 12 months.
- **Lifetime:** one payment covers your membership for good. You'll never see a renewal box again.

Your member card shows how far you're covered: **Paid through** followed by a month and year (for example "Paid through Sep 2027"), or **Lifetime member** for lifetime and honorary members.

## How is my paid-through date worked out?
- **First payment:** 12 months from the day you pay.
- **Renewing early or on time** (in the last 30 days before your paid-through date): 12 months are added to your current paid-through date, so you never lose any days and your renewal date never drifts. For example, if you're paid through October 4 and renew on September 20, you're paid through October 4 of the next year.
- **Renewing late:** your date rolls forward to the next anniversary after today. One payment catches you up, but you don't get bonus time for the months you were lapsed. For example, if you were paid through March and renew in May, you're paid through next March.

## Do Society dues renew automatically?
No. Nothing charges your card automatically. The Society page says it plainly: "Cancel or lapse any time. Nothing auto-charges without warning." To keep your membership, you pay again each year. The reminder emails say your membership "expires" on a date; they're reminders to pay, not a charge.

## When can I renew?
Renewal opens **30 days before** your paid-through date and stays open after that date passes. Once it's open:

- An amber notice appears at the top of every member-area page: "Your membership expires in [number] days, on [date]. Renewing early adds a full year on top, so you don't lose any days." (on the last day: "Your membership expires today."), with a **Renew now** button.
- The member area's Overview page shows a **Renew now** button under your member card.
- The reminder emails have a **Renew now** button too.

Every **Renew now** takes you to the renewal box on [the membership page](/c/underground-aquarium-society?renew=1#renew). Paying early never costs you days: the new year starts when your current one ends.

Before the 30-day window, there's no Pay button. If you open the renewal link too early, the page says "You're paid through [date]. Renewal opens 30 days before that date. We'll email you a reminder when it's time." Checkout also refuses an early payment with "You're paid through [date]. Renewal opens 30 days before that date."

Lifetime members, honorary members and the Society's owner never need to renew and don't see any of this.

## What renewal reminder emails will I get?
Individual members with a paid-through date get up to four emails per renewal cycle:

- **10 days before:** "Your Underground Aquarium Society membership expires in 10 days", with the amount and the **Expires on** date. It explains that you can renew now and that renewing early adds a full year on top of your current one, so you don't lose any days. Button: **Renew now**.
- **3 days before:** "Your Underground Aquarium Society membership expires in 3 days." Button: **Renew now**.
- **On the day:** "Your Underground Aquarium Society membership expires today." Button: **Renew now**.
- **3 days after:** "Your Underground Aquarium Society membership has lapsed", with the date it lapsed and a note that your records are safe. Button: **Renew your membership**.

Each **Renew now** button opens the renewal box on the membership page, where you can pay straight away.

Each reminder is sent once per cycle. Lifetime and honorary members don't get them.

## Do I get a receipt for my Society dues?
Yes. After a successful payment, a receipt email is sent to the email you paid with. The subject is "Your Underground Aquarium Society dues receipt." It shows the **Amount** and **Membership active through** date, and says "Keep this email as your receipt."

## What happens when my Society dues lapse?
When your paid-through date passes and you haven't renewed:

- **The whole member area locks.** That includes Overview, Breeder Program, Spawn Logs, My Submissions, Review Queue, Leaderboard, Certificates and Raffle. Any Society link sends you to [the membership page](/c/underground-aquarium-society), which says "Your membership lapsed on [date]. Renew to get back in." and shows the **Renew your membership** box with a **Renew for [amount]** button.
- **You can't open new spawn logs or add stages** until you renew.
- **The brass ring on your avatar goes away** until you're back in good standing.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Your spawn logs, points and certificates are kept and come back the moment your renewal clears. The page says: "Your spawn logs, points and certificates are all still here. They unlock again the moment your payment clears."
- **Your certificates still verify.** Anyone checking your certificate code at [the Certificate Registry](/verify) still sees the record while you're lapsed.
- **Your free account isn't affected.** Classifieds, messages, forums, feed and tools keep working.

Lifetime members, honorary members and Society officers never lock out.

## What does "Your membership lapsed on [date]. Renew to get back in." mean?
Your paid-through date has passed and you haven't renewed, so the member area is locked. You see this on [the membership page](/c/underground-aquarium-society), which is where Society links send you while you're lapsed. Click **Renew for [amount]** in the **Renew your membership** box just below it. Everything unlocks as soon as the payment clears.

## What does "Your membership expires in [number] days" mean?
You're in the last 30 days before your paid-through date, so renewal is open. This amber notice appears at the top of every member-area page. Click **Renew now** to pay for your next year. Renewing early adds a full year on top of your current paid-through date, so you don't lose any days. If you do nothing, your membership lapses when the date passes.

## How do I renew my Society membership?
1. Sign in. If you're in the last 30 days, click **Renew now** in the amber notice at the top of the member area, under your member card on Overview, or in a reminder email. If you're lapsed, click **The Society** in the menu and you land on the membership page automatically.
2. In the renewal box, check the "Paid through after this payment:" date, then click **Renew for [amount]**.
3. Complete Stripe checkout.

Everything unlocks as soon as the payment clears, and you'll get a receipt email.

## I cancelled at checkout. Was I charged?
No. If you close or back out of the Stripe checkout page, you return to the membership page and nothing is charged. You can click **Pay** again whenever you're ready.

## Can I get a refund on Society dues?
The site has no refund button. Email support@undergroundaquarium.com and include the email address you paid with.

## Is my card information safe?
Dues are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout page. Underground Aquarium never sees or stores your card number.

## Common problems
**"Couldn't start checkout." or "Something went wrong."** The checkout page couldn't be opened. Wait a moment and click **Pay** again. If it keeps failing, email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

**"This club isn't set up to collect dues yet."** The Society's payment account isn't ready to take payments at the moment. Email support@undergroundaquarium.com and we'll sort it out.

**"This club has no dues." or "This club has no dues for your membership type."** There's no price set for your plan. Email support@undergroundaquarium.com.

**"This club hasn't set a lifetime membership rate yet."** You're on the Lifetime plan but no Lifetime price is set. Email support@undergroundaquarium.com to switch plans or get help.

**"Apply to join first. Dues open up once you're approved."** Your application is still waiting for approval, so there's nothing to pay yet. You'll get a notification when you're approved. See [Joining the Society](/help/joining-the-society).

**"You're paid through [date]. Renewal opens 30 days before that date."** You tried to pay before renewal opened. Come back in the last 30 days before that date; we'll email you a reminder.

**"Online dues payments are paused for a moment."** The Society can't take payments right now. Try again later, or email support@undergroundaquarium.com and we'll sort it out.

**"Please sign in."** Your session ended. Sign in again, return to [the membership page](/c/underground-aquarium-society) and click **Pay**.

**I paid but the page still asks me to pay.** Payments are confirmed by Stripe a moment after checkout. Wait a minute and reload the membership page. If you still see the Pay box and you have a receipt email, email support@undergroundaquarium.com with the receipt.

**I got an "expires in 10 days" email. Can I pay now?** Yes. Click **Renew now** in the email. Renewing early adds a full year on top of your current paid-through date, so you don't lose any days.

**I want to renew but there's no Renew button.** Renewal only opens 30 days before your paid-through date. Until then, the renewal page tells you the date you're paid through.

**I was approved but there's no Pay button at all.** If the Society can't take payments at that moment, you see "Online dues payments are paused for a moment." instead. Try again later, or email support@undergroundaquarium.com.
