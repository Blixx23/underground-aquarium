Every tank you save in the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder) gets its own page. Make it public and it shows on your profile, appears in the feed as "shared a tank", and other keepers can vote on it and comment. You need to be signed in to save and share tanks.

## How do I share a tank publicly?
1. Open the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder) while signed in, and build your tank (size and stock) or pick one of your saved tanks under **Your tanks**.
2. In the save section (**3. Save your build**, or **3. Your saved tank** for one you've saved before), give it a name in the box "Name this tank (e.g. Living room 29)". Names can be up to 80 characters. If you leave it blank, it's named after its size (for example "29-gallon tank") or "My Tank".
3. Tick **Show this tank on my profile and in the feed**.
4. Tap **Save tank** (or **Update tank**). You'll see "Saved."
5. Tap **View public page →** to open the tank's page. This link appears once the tank is public and has no unsaved changes.

Your tank now shows on the **Tanks** tab of your public profile, in the [Feed](/feed) as "shared a tank", and on its own page. The first time you share a tank publicly you earn bubbles (see [Bubbles](/help/bubbles)).

For saving, the 4-tank limit and share links, see [Saving and sharing tanks](/help/saving-and-sharing-tanks).

## What's on a tank's page?
- **Community tank** label, the tank name, and a line of facts: size in gallons, number of species, number of fish and how long it's been running (for example "Running 1 year, 4 months").
- A photo gallery: a big cover photo with thumbnails. Tap to view full screen; swipe on a phone or use arrow keys on a computer.
- The keeper: "Kept by" and their name (or "Your tank" on your own), linking to their profile, with a gold seal for Society members.
- **Float up** and **Sink** vote buttons with the tank's score (see [Tank likes and comments](/help/tank-likes-and-comments)).
- **About this tank**: the story, if the owner wrote one.
- **What's in it**: every species with its scientific name and quantity (for example ×6). Each one links to its species page.
- **The setup**: when it was set up, its size and the gear list, if filled in.
- **Comments**, a **Plan your own tank** link to the Tank Builder and, for visitors, **Report this tank**.

## How do I add photos, a story and gear to my tank page?
On your tank's page, tap **Edit showcase** (only you see this button). A panel opens with four parts:

- **Photos**: up to 12 photos (see the next section).
- **About this tank**: the story behind it, up to 2,000 characters, with a counter. The placeholder suggests "What's the idea behind it? The scape, the stock, what you're proudest of, what went wrong along the way." The first line or two also shows on your tank's tile.
- **Set up on**: the date you started the tank. It can't be in the future. The page then shows how long it's been running.
- **The setup**: **Filter**, **Lighting**, **Substrate**, **Heater**, **CO₂**, **Fertilizer** and **Water**, up to 120 characters each. Examples are shown in each box, like "Fluval 307 canister". Leave anything blank and it won't show.

Tap **Save** to publish your changes, or **Cancel** to close without saving.

## How do tank photos work?
In **Edit showcase**, the photo section shows how many you have out of 12, with the note "first one is the cover".

- Tap **Add photos** to upload one or more. The note under the photos says "Each photo can be up to 10 MB. iPhone photos are converted and big photos are resized automatically." Big photos are resized to at most 2000 pixels on the longest side, and iPhone .heic files show up in the file picker on a computer.
- Use the left and right arrows on a photo (**Move earlier**, **Move later**) to reorder.
- Tap the star (**Make cover**) to move a photo to the front. The first photo has a **Cover** label and is used on your tank's tile, in the feed and when the page is shared.
- Tap the trash icon (**Remove photo**) to take one out.

Nothing is final until you tap **Save**. You can also add photos in the Tank Builder's save section. Both places accept the same photos, with the same 10 MB limit per photo and the same 12-photo limit.

## What do the photo and save messages mean?
- **Up to 12 photos per tank.** You've hit the limit. Remove one to add another.
- **Photos only, please.** A file you picked isn't an image.
- **That photo is too large (max 10 MB).** That photo was skipped. Pick a smaller version of it.
- **A photo didn't upload. Try that one again.** A connection hiccup. Add it again.
- **Couldn't save. Try again.** Your changes didn't save. Check your connection and tap **Save** again.

## How do I change the fish in my tank?
Tap **Edit stock** on your tank's page. It opens the tank in the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder), where you can add or remove species and change quantities. Tap **Update tank** there to save. The tank page updates with the new stock.

## Who can see my tank?
- **Public tanks** (with **Show this tank on my profile and in the feed** ticked) can be seen by anyone, signed in or not, and show on your profile and in the feed.
- **Private tanks** can only be seen by you. On your own private tank's page you'll see a lock badge saying **Only you can see this**. Other people can't open it.

On your own profile, your tank tiles are marked **Posted** or **Private**. Photos you add to a private tank only become visible to others once you make the tank public.

## How do I make a public tank private again?
1. Open it in the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder) (tap **Edit stock** on its page, or pick it under **Your tanks**).
2. Untick **Show this tank on my profile and in the feed**.
3. Tap **Update tank**.

It disappears from your profile's Tanks tab and other people can no longer open its page. Its votes and comments are kept if you make it public again later.

## How do I delete a tank?
In the [Tank Builder](/tank-builder), tap the **X** on the tank under **Your tanks** and confirm "Delete ... ? Its water test history goes with it." The tank, its page and its water test history are removed. This can't be undone. If it fails you'll see "Couldn't delete that tank."

## Where do my shared tanks show up?
- The **Tanks** tab of your public profile, as photo tiles with the name, size, species count and the start of your story. If you haven't shared any, visitors see that you haven't shared any tanks yet.
- The sidebar of your profile's Feed tab on larger screens.
- The [Feed](/feed), as "shared a tank".
- Its own page, which you can link to from anywhere.

## Common problems
**I don't see Edit showcase.** Only the tank's owner sees it, and only when signed in. Make sure you're signed in to the account that saved the tank.

**View public page doesn't appear.** It only shows when **Show this tank on my profile and in the feed** is ticked, the tank has been saved, and there are no unsaved changes. Tap **Update tank** first.

**My tank isn't on my profile.** It's private. Tick the public box in the Tank Builder and tap **Update tank**.

**My photos aren't showing to others.** The tank is private, or you added photos and closed **Edit showcase** without tapping **Save**.

**My iPhone photo won't upload.** iPhone HEIC photos are converted automatically, in any browser. If one still fails, you'll see a message that it's an iPhone HEIC photo this browser can't read. On your iPhone, **Settings › Camera › Formats › Most Compatible** saves new photos as JPEG, or share the photo to yourself first so it comes through as a JPEG.

**I can't save another tank.** You can keep up to 4 saved tanks. Delete or reuse one. See [Saving and sharing tanks](/help/saving-and-sharing-tanks).
