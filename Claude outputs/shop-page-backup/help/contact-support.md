Something not working, or have an idea to make the site better? Search the [Help Center](/help) first, and if you still need a hand, email us. A real person reads every message.

## How do I contact Underground Aquarium support?
Email support@undergroundaquarium.com. It's the one support address for everything: account questions, bugs, a store listing you manage, Society questions, privacy requests and ideas.

You can start an email from:

- the **Email support** button in the **Still stuck?** box at the bottom of the [Help Center](/help) and every help guide
- the envelope icon in the footer of every page
- the "no results" message in the help search box

## Is there a phone number or live chat?
No. Email is the only way to reach support. There's no phone line, live chat or contact form.

## What should I include in my email?
A few details help us sort things out on the first reply:

- **The link** to the page, listing, thread or store you're asking about
- **What you were trying to do**, and what happened instead
- **Any error message**, word for word (or a screenshot)
- **Your device and browser**, for example "iPhone, Safari" or "Windows laptop, Chrome"
- **Your username**, and send from the email address on your account if it's about your account
- **The build code** from the very bottom of any page (it looks like "build" followed by a short code), if you're reporting a bug

A screenshot is often the quickest way to show us. Attach it if you can.

## When should I email support instead of using the site?
Email us for things you can't do yourself, such as:

- you can't log in and a password reset isn't working
- changing the email address on your account
- merging or removing a duplicate account
- editing something the site doesn't let you edit, like a forum post
- a Society membership, dues or certificate question
- a problem with a store page you own
- a bug, a broken page or something that looks wrong
- privacy requests about your data
- suggestions and feature ideas

## How do I report a listing, post or person?
If a classified ad, post, comment or member looks like a scam, breaks the rules or makes you uncomfortable, use the report option on the site instead of emailing. Reports go straight to our team with the right details attached. See [Reporting content](/help/reporting-content). To stop someone contacting you, see [Blocking people](/help/blocking-people).

If you think you're in danger or have been the victim of a crime, contact your local authorities first.

## How do I fix wrong details on a fish store page?
Use **Suggest a fix** on the store's page to report wrong hours, a wrong address or a closed shop. To add a store we're missing, see [Suggesting a store](/help/suggesting-a-store). Store owners can claim their page and edit it directly: see [Claiming your store](/help/claiming-your-store).

## Can I reply to an email from Underground Aquarium?
Emails we send about your account and activity generally answer to our support inbox, so replying reaches us. If an email names a different reply address (for example a club's contact), your reply goes there instead. To stop outreach, use the unsubscribe link in the email; to stop shop alerts, use the settings link. See [Emails we send](/help/emails-we-send).

## How long does it take to hear back?
We're a small team and answer as soon as we can. Check your spam folder for our reply. Sending the details listed above the first time usually saves a round of back and forth.

## Is there a feedback button on the site?
No. There's no feedback button or form on the site right now. Send ideas and feedback by email to support@undergroundaquarium.com. We read everything.

## Common problems

**My email to support bounced.**
Check the spelling: support@undergroundaquarium.com (under-ground-aquarium, all one word, no hyphens).

**I can't log in, so I can't reach my account to ask for help.**
You don't need to be logged in to email us. Send your username and the email address you signed up with.

**I emailed but haven't heard back.**
Check spam and promotions folders. If it's been a while, reply to your original email so your details stay together.

**Tapping the email link or button does nothing.**
It opens your device's default mail app. If you don't have one set up, copy the address support@undergroundaquarium.com into whatever email you use.
