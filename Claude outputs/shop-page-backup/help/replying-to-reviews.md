A short, friendly reply to every review shows shoppers there's a real person behind the counter. You can reply to each review on your shop once, and edit or delete your reply any time.

## Where do I see my shop's reviews?
Open **Reviews** in your [shop dashboard](/help/shop-dashboard-overview). The page says "A short, friendly reply to every review is the cheapest advertising there is. The reviewer gets a notification when you answer." Below that are all your reviews, newest first, with the count and average at the top.

A yellow number on the **Reviews** tab shows how many reviews don't have a reply yet. [My shops](/my/shops) shows the same thing as "[N] reviews to answer".

You'll also find every review, with the same reply tools, on your public page under **Reviews**.

## How do I reply to a review?
1. Find the review and tap **Respond** under it.
2. Write your reply in the box that says "Reply as the owner…".
3. Tap **Save response** (it says **Saving…**), or **Cancel**.

Your reply appears under the review in a green box labeled **Owner's response**. **Save response** stays grayed out until you've written something.

## What does the reviewer see when I reply?
The reviewer gets an in-app notification titled "[Shop] responded" that says "The shop replied to your review. Tap to see what they said." It opens your shop's page. Everyone else sees your reply under the review, labeled **Owner's response**. Your personal name isn't shown on the reply.

## How do I edit or delete my reply?
Next to **Owner's response**, tap the pencil icon to edit it (then **Save response**), or the trash can icon to delete it. Deleting is immediate, with no confirmation. Once deleted, the **Respond** link comes back so you can write a new one.

If you edit a reply soon after posting it, the reviewer may get the "responded" notification again.

## How many times can I reply to a review?
One reply per review. Editing replaces it. There are no back-and-forth threads: the reviewer can't answer your reply on the site.

## How do I know when I get a new review?
You get an alert in your notifications and an email with the subject "New [N]-star review for [shop]". The email shows the stars, the reviewer's name and the start of what they wrote, with a **Reply to this review** button that opens your **Reviews** tab. You can switch these off under **Your shop: new reviews** in your notification settings. See [Shop notifications and emails](/help/shop-notifications-and-emails).

## Can I delete or hide a review?
No. Owners can reply but can't edit, hide or remove reviews. Only the person who wrote a review can change or delete it.

## What should I do about an unfair or fake review?
1. Reply calmly and briefly. Shoppers read replies, and a fair answer often matters more than the review.
2. If the review breaks the [community rules](/help/community-rules) (it's abusive, it's spam, it's about a different shop, or it's clearly not a real customer), email support@undergroundaquarium.com with your shop's name and the reviewer's name. Our team will look at it. There's no report button on reviews yet.

## Can I review my own shop?
No. While you manage a shop, you'll see "You manage this store, so you can't review it yourself, but you can respond to reviews above." instead of the review form.

## Tips for good replies
- Thank people by name for good reviews, and mention something specific they said.
- For complaints, acknowledge it, say what you've changed, and invite them back or to call the shop.
- Keep it short. Two or three sentences is plenty.
- Don't share a customer's personal details in a reply.

## How do reviews affect my shop's rating?
Your rating is the plain average of all reviews' stars, shown to one decimal place. Replies don't change the rating. Your Overview's **Rating** card shows your average, total reviews and how many are new in the last 7 or 30 days.

## Common problems
**I don't see a Respond link.** You're either not signed in with the account that manages the shop, or the review already has a reply (use the pencil icon to edit it).

**The yellow number didn't go down after I replied.** Refresh the page. The count updates when the page reloads.

**My reply didn't save.** If the reply box is still open after you tap **Save response**, the reply didn't go through, and no error message is shown. Check your connection and tap **Save response** again.

**I don't see "Edited" on reviews in my dashboard.** The "· Edited" label shows on your public page. Tap **View public page** to see it.

**A review is about a different shop.** Email support@undergroundaquarium.com with the details.
