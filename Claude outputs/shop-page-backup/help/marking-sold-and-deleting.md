When your item is gone, mark the ad as sold so people stop messaging you. If you never want it back, you can delete it. Marking sold can be undone; deleting can't.

## How do I mark a listing as sold?
You can do it from two places.

### From your listing page
1. Open your ad (tap it in [My listings](/my/listings) or anywhere on the site).
2. Tap **Mark as sold** (for Wanted ads, **Mark as found**).
3. Your browser asks you to confirm: "Mark as sold? It comes off the marketplace, and you can relist it from My Listings." Tap **OK**.

The page refreshes and shows a banner that the ad is now sold and only you can see it.

### From My listings
1. Open [My listings](/my/listings).
2. Tap **Mark sold** (for Wanted ads, **Mark found**) on the active ad.

There's no confirmation step here: the ad is marked sold straight away.

## What happens when I mark something sold?
- The ad comes off area pages, the [Every listing](/listings) page, search and your public profile right away.
- Its status becomes **sold** in [My listings](/my/listings). Wanted ads marked found also show as sold.
- Other people who open its link get a "page not found" page. You can still open it.
- Buyers can no longer start a new conversation about it.
- Conversations that already exist stay in both people's [Messages](/messages). Under the ad title, the conversation shows the price followed by "· no longer listed". You can both keep messaging.
- Nothing is deleted, and your views are kept.

The site doesn't send any message to people who asked about the ad, so if you promised someone a reply, let them know yourself.

## Can I mark an ad as "pending" or "on hold"?
No. The only statuses are active and sold. If a sale is pending, leave the ad active and add "Pending pickup" to the start of the title or description with **Edit**. Mark it sold once it's done.

## I marked it sold by mistake. Can I undo it?
Yes.
1. Open [My listings](/my/listings).
2. Tap **Repost** on the sold ad.

It goes live again right away with a fresh 45 days and moves to the top of Newest first. The link, photos, views and conversations are unchanged. The same **Repost** button works if the sale falls through days later.

## How do I delete a listing?
1. Open [My listings](/my/listings).
2. Tap **Delete** on the ad.
3. Tap **Delete for good** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep it.

Deleting works on any ad, whatever its status. There's no delete button on the listing page itself; it's only in My listings.

## What happens when I delete an ad?
- The ad is gone for good. It disappears from My listings, and its link shows "page not found" for everyone, including you.
- It can't be undone or restored. You'd need to post it again from scratch.
- Existing conversations about it stay in [Messages](/messages). In your inbox and at the top of the conversation, they show "Listing removed" instead of the ad title.

## Should I mark sold or delete?
- **Mark sold** if there's any chance you'll want the ad back, you have more of the same thing coming, or you want to keep the photos and description to repost later.
- **Delete** if you posted by mistake, posted a duplicate, or never want it again.

Marking sold is usually the better choice. Sold ads are only visible to you, so they don't clutter the classifieds.

## Can I delete a whole batch of ads at once?
No. Each ad is deleted one at a time with **Delete** and **Delete for good**. If you want to remove all your content, see [Deleting your account](/help/deleting-your-account).

## Does anyone see that my item sold?
No. There's no public "sold" badge. A sold ad simply disappears from the classifieds and your profile. Only people already talking to you about it see "no longer listed" in their conversation.

## Common problems

**I don't see a Mark as sold button on my ad.**
It only shows on **active** ads, and only to you while signed in. If the ad is already sold, expired or removed, the button isn't there. Check the status in [My listings](/my/listings).

**"Couldn't update it. Try again."**
Marking sold from the listing page didn't save. Reload the page and try again.

**"That didn't save. Try again."**
An action in My listings didn't go through. Reload [My listings](/my/listings) and try again.

**People are still messaging me about a sold item.**
Check the ad shows **sold** in [My listings](/my/listings). People already in a conversation can keep replying there; let them know it's gone. New buyers can't start a conversation about a sold ad.

**I deleted an ad by accident.**
Deleted ads can't be restored. Post it again from [Post it free](/post). Next time, mark it sold instead, which can be undone with **Repost**.

**I can't find Delete on the listing page.**
Delete is only in [My listings](/my/listings). From your listing page, tap **Manage this listing** to get there.
